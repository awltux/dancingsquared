A duplicate is a definition whose **setup** — shape, where the genders stand **and** the order the
dancers are listed in — is already carried by another definition, so it is the same setup under a second
spelling. The ids come from the geometry (`reconstituteWholeSet` of the written half), and a `refactor`
row is closed by making the call name the `formations.xml` definition instead of carrying its own copy of
the geometry.

The tags refine one another where BOTH are rooted in the same definition: `fid` names the shape, `aid`
adds where the genders stand, `sid` adds the order. `sid` is minted from the definition's own board, while
`fid`/`aid` may be inherited from a near-twin at tolerance, so 9 definition(s) here carry an
`aid` that is not the arrangement their `sid` embeds.

227 definition(s) over 56 arrangement(s) share an arrangement with another
definition but list the dancers in a different ORDER: those are different setups (their couples are
numbered differently), so they are deliberately NOT rows in this table.
