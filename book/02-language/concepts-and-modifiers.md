# Concepts and modifiers

Square dancers have a small set of words that change how a call applies, without being calls
themselves. These are the ones the Mainstream program uses.

## Naming a subset: Split, Each, Center, Ends

| Word | Means |
|---|---|
| **Split** | divide the named group in two and work each half separately. *Split Circulate*, *Split Two* |
| **Each Side** | apply the call to the two sides of the formation separately |
| **Center Four** | the four dancers nearest the middle |
| **Outside Four** | the four dancers outside them |
| **Center 6 / Outside 6** | the same for six and two |
| **Very Centers / Very Ends** | the two dancers nearest the middle, or the outermost two |

## All 8

**All 8** means *everybody does this*. [All 8 Circulate](../04-calls/m19-circulate-family.md) is the
common example.

The manual adds a warning worth repeating, because it is regularly taught wrongly:

> "It is improper to teach that All 8 Circulate always means 'ends stay ends and centers stay
> centers.' This statement is true from Ocean Waves and Two-Faced Lines, but not from Columns."

## Nothing

Some calls with a qualifier can be qualified with **Nothing**, meaning that part is skipped. It is a
caller's device for keeping dancers awake, and you will meet it sooner or later in "an *Anything*
figure".

## The *Anything* family

A large part of square dance calling works by **substitution**: the caller says a call with
"*anything*" in it, and whatever they then say is slotted into that position.

> **"Explode and *Anything*"** — everybody explodes, then does whatever the caller names next.

At Mainstream you meet **[Clover and *Anything*](../07-plus/plus-programme.md)** shapes and the
[Plus](../07-plus/where-mainstream-leads.md) *Anything* calls; the idea is the same: a wrapper call with
a slot in it.

The manual defines these by saying the dancers complete the first part, then complete the call named
in the slot.

## Those Who Can

**"Those who can"** means: if the described action is possible for you from where you are, do it; if
it is not, you do nothing.

It is a way of writing a call that might or might not apply to everyone, without forcing the caller
to name anybody. You will meet it in the manual's comments more than in Mainstream choreography.

## Fold, Run, Trade, Circulate — families, not modifiers

Four words that behave like modifiers but are calls in their own right, because they always need to
be applied to *somebody*:

- **Fold** — one dancer folds behind the other, turning to face the same way — [M48](../04-calls/m48-fold-cross-fold.md)
- **Run** — one dancer runs around the adjacent dancer — [M34](../04-calls/m34-run-cross-run.md)
- **Trade** — two dancers exchange places — [M32](../04-calls/m32-trade-family.md)
- **Circulate** — dancers move forward to the next position on a path — [M19](../04-calls/m19-circulate-family.md)

Each has a family page with the ways it is named.
