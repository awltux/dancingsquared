# Circle Rule

A caller is not restricted to calling out of lines and waves. The Circle Rule covers the case where
the dancers are **circling**, and the caller names four of them and gives a call that wants Facing
Couples.

> "From an Infacing Circle of 8, if the caller names two adjacent dancers and their opposites and
> gives a call that starts from Facing Couples, the dancers dance the call **as if the Circle has
> been changed into a Static Square**, and follow the [Squared Set Convention](squared-set-convention.md)."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

## What it means in practice

The circle stops being a circle for the purposes of that call. The named four dancers step in and
dance the call from where they are, treating the circle exactly as though it were a squared set.

The manual's own examples:

> "From a Squared Set: 'Join Hands, Circle Left; **Heads Up to the Middle and Back; Heads Square Thru
> 4**.'"

> "From a Squared Set: 'Heads Half Sashay; All Circle Left; **Four Boys Spin the Top**.' (After the
> Heads Half Sashay the Circle will be BBGGBBGG. For Four Boys Spin the Top the four Boys begin by
> stepping forward to make a Wave in the Center while the Girls form Couples perpendicular to that
> Wave.)"

That second example is worth reading twice, because it shows how much the rule is doing: the circle
has an unusual gender arrangement (BBGGBBGG, not the usual alternating), the caller names four
dancers who are not a standard pair of couples, and it still works — because the Circle Rule turns
the circle into a squared set for the purpose of the call.

## Why it exists

Because a caller often wants to start a figure from a circle rather than stopping the music to
rebuild a square. Without the rule, the same call would be improper from a circle, and the caller
would have to spend a phrase getting everyone into lines first.

## What you need to know as a dancer

1. If you are **not named**, keep circling — or, more often, the caller has already stopped the
   circle and you hold your position.
2. If you **are** named, step in and dance it exactly as if you were in a squared set.
3. Follow the [Squared Set Convention](squared-set-convention.md) for where you finish.
