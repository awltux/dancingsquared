# Passing Rule

> "If the definition of a call has two dancers on the same path and requires them to walk past each
> other, they should pass **right shoulders** (unless otherwise specified) and continue."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

This is the single most useful rule in square dancing. It answers the question "what happens when we
both walk at the same spot?" without the caller having to say.

## What it means in practice

When you are walking toward another dancer and there is not room for both of you on the same line:

1. **Pass right shoulders** — your right shoulder goes past their right shoulder.
2. **Keep going.** Do not stop, do not dodge, do not improvise a way round.
3. **Do not turn it into a turn.** A pass is a pass; you do not take hands.

Right shoulders by default, because most dancers are right-handed and the movement is natural.
The mirror version — a Left pass, as in
[Left Square Thru](../04-calls/m21-square-thru.md) — requires the caller to say so.

## Calls that are built on it

The Passing Rule is what makes these work:

- [Pass Thru](../04-calls/m07-pass-thru.md) — the plainest case
- [Dosado](../04-calls/m02-dosado.md) — a pass on both shoulders, in sequence
- [Right and Left Grand](../04-calls/m06-right-and-left-grand-family.md) — a continuous series of passes
- [Square Thru](../04-calls/m21-square-thru.md) — four passes in a row, alternating hands
- [Double Pass Thru](../04-calls/m24-double-pass-thru.md) — everyone passes, twice over

## The directional warning

The rule has a sharp edge that catches dancers out: it applies to **two dancers on the same path**.
When two dancers are moving in opposite directions along the same path — which happens constantly in
circulates — they pass **right shoulders**, and the call expects that.

But once you are holding hands with someone, the Passing Rule is not in play. A joined pair moving
past another joined pair uses the handhold, not the shoulders. The rule is about walking past, not
about dancing through.

## Interaction with the Same Position Rule

The two rules answer different questions and are easy to confuse:

| Situation | Rule |
|---|---|
| Two dancers walking past each other | [Passing Rule](#) — right shoulders |
| Two dancers ending on the same spot | [Same Position Rule](same-position-rule.md) — form a right-hand mini-wave |

## For teachers

This is worth teaching on the first night, before any call that needs it. A class that has not been
told the Passing Rule will invent its own, differently, and then spend months undoing it.
