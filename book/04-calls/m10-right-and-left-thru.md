# M10 — Right and Left Thru

> **Teaching unit M10** of the CALLERLAB (New) Mainstream Suggested Teaching Order, effective
> 1 September 2026. Prerequisites: [Facing Couples](../02-language/formations.md#facing-couples),
> [Right Pull By](../02-language/hands-and-holds.md), [Courtesy Turn](m09-courtesy-turn.md).

## Right and Left Thru

### At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Starting formation** | [Facing Couples](../02-language/formations.md#facing-couples) |
| **Ending formation** | [Facing Couples](../02-language/formations.md#facing-couples) — the same two couples, swapped over |
| **Timing** | 6 beats |
| **Unit** | M10 of 50 |

### Animation

▶ **Play this call** — pause, step and scrub it: [Watching the animations](../08-reference/watching-the-animations.md).

### Dance action

> "Right Pull By; Courtesy Turn"

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

### What you do

Two couples are facing each other. You are going to walk past the facing dancer and then turn your own
partner round.

1. **Take right hands with the dancer facing you** and pass them, letting go as you go by. It is a
   [Right Pull By](../02-language/hands-and-holds.md) — a light touch, not a tug. The manual says a
   literal pull "is neither required nor desired".
2. **Slide past and find your own partner** on the far side. You and your partner are now side by
   side, facing out, away from the couple you just passed.
3. **Courtesy Turn with your partner.** Join left hands and turn as a unit until you are facing the
   middle again — see [M9](m09-courtesy-turn.md) for the full hold.
4. **Finish as [Facing Couples](../02-language/formations.md#facing-couples)**: the two couples are facing each other
   again, but each dancer has swapped to the other side of the set.

The whole call is one smooth movement: pass, meet your partner, turn, face in. Six beats.

### Timing

**6 beats** — about four beats of walking through the middle and two beats of turning. If you find
yourself waiting for your partner, you passed too fast; if you collide, you passed too close.

### Styling

> "Dancers extend right hands to each other, and perform a Pass Thru action, releasing handholds as
> they pass each other. A literal "pull" is neither required nor desired."

Everything about the second half is [Courtesy Turn](m09-courtesy-turn.md) styling: left hands joined,
palm down on palm up, right hands light and free.

### Restrictions

At Mainstream the Courtesy Turn in this call is **a Boy turning a Girl**, so Right and Left Thru needs
**Normal Couples**. From [Facing Lines](../02-language/formations.md#lines) where only the centre dancers
form a Normal Couple, the manual is explicit: the caller must not say "Right and Left Thru" hoping
that only the centres will do it — the proper call is "**Centers Right and Left Thru**".

### Common faults

| What happens | Why | The fix |
|---|---|---|
| You end up facing the wrong couple | Turning before you reach your partner | Pass the facing dancer first, then find your own partner and turn |
| The pull becomes a wrestling match | Gripping the hand and hauling | Touch and release. The momentum comes from your feet |
| You finish beside your partner instead of facing in | Skipping the Courtesy Turn | Right Pull By **then** Courtesy Turn. Both halves are required |
| The two couples end up too close | Passing down the middle too slowly | Walk through at a steady pace and finish a comfortable distance apart |

### Where you meet it

[Class 3](../05-course/class-03.md). Right and Left Thru is the standard way to send two facing
couples past each other, and it appears in almost every tip from here on — often as a caller's
favourite way to fill eight beats without moving anybody far.

### Related calls

- **[Courtesy Turn](m09-courtesy-turn.md)** — the second half of this call, taught on its own.
- **[Pass Thru](m07-pass-thru.md)** — the straight-through move without hands or turn.
- **[Square Thru](m21-square-thru.md)** — four pull-bys instead of one.
- **[Ladies Chain](m12-ladies-chain-family.md)** — the Ladies cross instead of the partners
  swapping.

### Also called / formerly

You will hear "**Right and Left Through**" and, in some clubs, "**Right and Left**" on its own.
A "**Right and Left Thru and a Quarter More**" adds another quarter turn and ends in a Right-Hand
Two-Faced Line — that is the same call with a stated extra amount.
