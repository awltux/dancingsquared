# M12 — Ladies Chain Family

> **Teaching unit M12** of the CALLERLAB (New) Mainstream Suggested Teaching Order, effective
> 1 September 2026. Prerequisites: [Facing Couples](../02-language/formations.md#facing-couples),
> [Couple handhold](../02-language/hands-and-holds.md),
> [Star Right/Left](../04-calls/m05-star-right-left.md),
> [Courtesy Turn](../04-calls/m09-courtesy-turn.md).

## Ladies Chain, (Ladies Chain 3/4)

### At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Starting formation** | [Facing Couples](../02-language/formations.md#facing-couples) — a Normal Couple facing a Normal Couple |
| **Ending formation** | [Facing Couples](../02-language/formations.md#facing-couples), with the Girls swapped |
| **Timing** | 6 beats from Facing Couples; 8 from a Static Square |
| **Unit/part** | M12a of 50 |

### Animation

▶ **Play this call** — pause, step and scrub it: [Watching the animations](../08-reference/watching-the-animations.md).

### Dance action

> "Women step toward each other and Right-Hand Star 1/2 (or Right Pull By). Each Man Courtesy Turns
> the Woman coming toward him to end in Facing Couples."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

### What you do

1. **Girls, step toward the middle** and join right hands with the Girl opposite.
2. **Turn a half turn by the right** — a small right-hand star of two — and let go. (If there are
   only two of you chaining, this is a **right pull by**: take right hands and walk past each other.)
3. **Boys, step to your right** as your own partner leaves, so that the Girl coming toward you has
   somewhere to land.
4. **Boys, take the Girl in a [Courtesy Turn](../04-calls/m09-courtesy-turn.md)** — left hands joined
   in front, right hands joined behind — and turn her to face the couple you came from.
5. **Let go and re-form a [couple handhold](../02-language/hands-and-holds.md).** You are now facing
   the same two dancers, but each Boy has the other's partner.

The call is finished when both new couples are standing still and facing each other. **Ladies Chain
3/4** is the same call with the Girls turning three-quarters in the star instead of a half, which
sends them further round the set.

### Timing

**6 beats** from facing couples, **8 beats** from a Static Square. The star or pull by is the quick
part; the [Courtesy Turn](../04-calls/m09-courtesy-turn.md) is where the music tells you to slow
down.

### Styling

> "Each Man releases his partner and sidesteps to the right while turning his right shoulder slightly
> toward the center of the formation blending into the Courtesy Turn. Women use skirtwork for the
> Courtesy Turn (and in the Star, if used)."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

### Restrictions

The manual says **"Two Ladies Chain is improper if either Couple is sashayed or same-gender."** A
[Courtesy Turn](../04-calls/m09-courtesy-turn.md) is a Boy turning a Girl under a particular
handhold, so the call needs a Normal Couple on each side. When the square is not arranged that way
the caller uses a different call.

### Common faults

| What happens | Why | The fix |
|---|---|---|
| You finish facing your own original partner | You walked past the Girl coming toward you instead of taking her | Boys: after the star, the Girl you turn is the one **coming toward you**, not the one you started beside |
| The two Boys collide | Neither stepped aside after the star | Boys sidestep right as soon as their partner leaves, then blend into the Courtesy Turn |
| The star wobbles and stalls | Gripping too tightly and leaning back | A star of two is light — palms only, no pulling |
| The Girls' skirtwork gets in the way | Both hands used for the turn | Skirt in the left hand only if it is comfortable; the handhold always comes first |

### Where you meet it

Ladies Chain is taught in unit M12, in [Class 3](../05-course/class-03.md) of
[the course](../05-course/course-overview.md). It is the first call that moves dancers across the
square and back, and callers use it constantly to change who is standing beside whom.

### Related calls

- **[Right and Left Thru](m10-right-and-left-thru.md)** — the same hand action, but the Boys do the
  crossing.
- **[Courtesy Turn](m09-courtesy-turn.md)** — the half of this call you must know first.
- [Four Ladies Chain](#four-ladies-chain-chain-34) — every Girl chains at once. See part b below.
- [Chain Down the Line](#chain-down-the-line) — the chain done from a line. See part c below.

### Also called / formerly

**"Two Ladies Chain"**, **"Head Ladies Chain"**, **"Head Ladies Chain Across"** and **"Ladies Chain
Across"** all mean this call. Older material says *Ladies* where the 2026 manual says **Girls**.

## Four Ladies Chain, (Chain 3/4)

### At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Starting formation** | [Static Square](../01-start/the-square.md) of Normal Couples, or an infacing circle of four Normal Couples |
| **Ending formation** | [Static Square](../01-start/the-square.md) of Normal Couples |
| **Timing** | 8 beats; 10 for the 3/4 version |
| **Unit/part** | M12b of 50 |

### Animation

▶ **Play this call** — pause, step and scrub it: [Watching the animations](../08-reference/watching-the-animations.md).

### Dance action

> "All four women Right-Hand Star 1/2 (or 3/4, if designated). All Courtesy Turn to face the center
> of the set."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

### What you do

1. **Girls, step into the middle** and make a four-handed right-hand star.
2. **Turn the star a half turn** — or three-quarters, if the caller says "3/4" — and let go as you
   meet the Boy who is now in front of you.
3. **Take that Boy in a [Courtesy Turn](../04-calls/m09-courtesy-turn.md)** and finish facing the
   middle of the square.
4. **Let go and re-form a [couple handhold](../02-language/hands-and-holds.md).**

Every Girl has moved one position round the square, so every Boy has a new partner for the moment.
**Four Ladies Chain; Chain Them Home** (or **Chain Those Ladies Over and Back**) is the call done
twice, which puts everybody back where they started.

### Timing

**8 beats**, or **10 beats** for the 3/4 version. The extra beats in the 3/4 version are the extra
quarter of the star.

### Styling

> "Women use a palm or finger-tip star. … each Lady to pick up and hold a bit of the right edge of
> her skirt, or an arm's length of a long skirt, as she reaches into the star."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

The star is a **palm or fingertip star**, not a forearm grip — four dancers turning quickly, not a
wrestling match. The Boys use the same styling as in [Ladies Chain](#ladies-chain-ladies-chain-34).

### Restrictions

As with Ladies Chain, the manual states that **"Four Ladies Chain is improper if any Couple is
sashayed or same-gender."** Every [Courtesy Turn](../04-calls/m09-courtesy-turn.md) needs a Boy and
a Girl.

### Common faults

| What happens | Why | The fix |
|---|---|---|
| The star falls apart | Four dancers pulling in different directions | Palms or fingertips only, and turn together on the same beat |
| You finish facing out of the square | The Courtesy Turn was left half done | The turn finishes facing the **centre** of the set |
| You end up beside your own original partner | The star stopped short, or went too far | Half means half: meet the Boy opposite, not the one next door |
| Everybody drifts towards the middle | No one is holding their own weight | Keep the star in one place; your own feet carry you round |

### Where you meet it

Four Ladies Chain is taught in unit M12, in [Class 3](../05-course/class-03.md) of
[the course](../05-course/course-overview.md). It is the standard way to give everybody a new partner
without leaving the squared set, and it opens a great many singing calls.

### Related calls

- [Ladies Chain](#ladies-chain-ladies-chain-34) — the two-couple version. See part a above.
- **Right and Left Grand** — the other way to move everyone round the square. See
  [M6](m06-right-and-left-grand-family.md).
- **Courtesy Turn** — the finishing move, shared with every call in this family. See
  [M9](m09-courtesy-turn.md).

### Also called / formerly

**"All Four Ladies Chain"**, **"Four Ladies Chain Across"**, **"Chain Those Ladies Over and Back"**
and **"Four Ladies Chain; Chain Them Home"** all describe versions of this call.

## Chain Down the Line

### At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Starting formation** | a general [line](../02-language/formations.md#lines) with the Centres holding right hands |
| **Ending formation** | [Facing Couples](../02-language/formations.md#facing-couples) |
| **Timing** | 8 beats |
| **Unit/part** | M12c of 50 |

### Animation

▶ **Play this call** — pause, step and scrub it: [Watching the animations](../08-reference/watching-the-animations.md).

### Dance action

> "Centers Turn 1/2 by the Right while ends adjust as necessary. Then the ends Courtesy Turn the
> centers to finish in Facing Couples."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

### What you do

1. **The two dancers in the middle**, a Boy and a Girl holding right hands, **turn a half turn by
   the right** — a [right arm turn](../04-calls/m04-arm-turn-family.md) — and let go.
2. **The two on the ends step sideways** to make room for the middle dancers to come out.
3. **Each end takes the middle dancer who comes to them in a [Courtesy Turn](../04-calls/m09-courtesy-turn.md).**
4. **Finish in [Facing Couples](../02-language/formations.md#facing-couples).** Each middle dancer has swapped ends
   of the line.

At Mainstream the Girls are always the middle dancers and the Boys the ends, because a
[Courtesy Turn](../04-calls/m09-courtesy-turn.md) is a Boy turning a Girl.

### Timing

**8 beats.** The right arm turn is quick; the two [Courtesy Turns](../04-calls/m09-courtesy-turn.md)
take the rest.

### Styling

The manual gives Chain Down the Line no separate styling of its own; it uses the styling of the
[Courtesy Turn](../04-calls/m09-courtesy-turn.md) that finishes it.

> "Women use skirtwork for the Courtesy Turn."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

Keep the Courtesy Turn handhold — left hands joined in front, right hands joined behind — and keep
the ends of the line steady while the centres turn.

### Restrictions

The manual is explicit: **"At Mainstream, Chain Down the Line starts only with women as centers and
men as ends because Courtesy Turn is restricted to men turning women."** If the caller wants the Boys
in the middle, this is not the call.

### Common faults

| What happens | Why | The fix |
|---|---|---|
| The middle dancers walk instead of turning | The right arm turn was skipped | Join right forearms and really turn the half turn before letting go |
| The ends are in the way | Nobody adjusted sideways | Ends step out of the path as the middle dancers turn |
| You finish as a line, not facing couples | The Courtesy Turn was not completed | Turn the incoming dancer all the way round to face the other couple |
| The Boys try to be centers | The call was mis-heard | At Mainstream, centers are the Girls |

### Where you meet it

Chain Down the Line is taught in unit M12, in [Class 3](../05-course/class-03.md) of
[the course](../05-course/course-overview.md), once you know
[Courtesy Turn](../04-calls/m09-courtesy-turn.md). It is the family's way of working from a line
rather than from facing couples.

### Related calls

- [Ladies Chain](#ladies-chain-ladies-chain-34) — the same star-then-turn idea from facing couples. See part a above.
- **Courtesy Turn** — the finishing move. See [M9](m09-courtesy-turn.md).
- **Bend the Line** — the other common call from a two-faced line. See [M20](m20-bend-the-line.md).

### Also called / formerly

You will hear **"Ladies Chain Down the Line"**; it is the same call. Older material says *Ladies* and
*Men* where the 2026 manual says **Girls** and **Boys**.
