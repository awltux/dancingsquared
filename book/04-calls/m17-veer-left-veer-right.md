# M17 — Veer Left / Veer Right

> **Teaching unit M17** of the CALLERLAB (New) Mainstream Suggested Teaching Order, effective
> 1 September 2026. Prerequisites: [Facing Couples](../02-language/formations.md#facing-couples),
> [Two-Faced Line](../02-language/formations.md#lines),
> [Couple handhold](../02-language/hands-and-holds.md),

## Veer Left / Veer Right

### At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Starting formation** | [Facing Couples](../02-language/formations.md#facing-couples) or a [Two-Faced Line](../02-language/formations.md#lines) |
| **Ending formation** | a [Two-Faced Line](../02-language/formations.md#lines) from Facing Couples; [Back-to-Back Couples](../02-language/formations.md#back-to-back-couples) from a Two-Faced Line |
| **Timing** | 2 beats |
| **Unit** | M17 of 50 |

### Animation

▶ **Play this call** — pause, step and scrub it: [Watching the animations](../08-reference/watching-the-animations.md).

### Dance action

> "From Facing Couples, each Couple works as a unit and moves forward and in the given direction to
> finish in a Two-Faced Line. From a Two-Faced Line, the given direction must be toward the center of
> the Line. Each Couple works as a unit and moves forward and in the given direction to finish in
> Back-to-Back Couples."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

### What you do

You and your partner are a couple, holding hands. You are facing another couple.

1. **Both keep walking forward** — nobody turns in place.
2. **Slide diagonally in the given direction as you go.** A **Veer Left** takes the whole couple
   forward and to the left, together. A **Veer Right** takes the couple forward and to the right.
3. **Finish side by side with your partner, still facing the same general way**, and now lined up
   beside the other couple. That shape is a [Two-Faced Line](../02-language/formations.md#lines): two
   couples back to back, each facing out.

If you start **from** a Two-Faced Line instead, the same walking action applies, but the named
direction must be the one that takes you **towards the middle of the line**. You finish as
[Back-to-Back Couples](../02-language/formations.md#back-to-back-couples) — each couple facing a couple, not lined up with
one.

The whole call is two beats: it is a small diagonal step, not a journey.

### Timing

**2 beats.** Veer is one of the shortest calls in the program — the equivalent of a single beat of
travel each way.

### Styling

> "All dancers use Couple handhold. Outside hands in normal dance position."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

The couple handhold stays joined throughout. Keep the couple's shoulders parallel — the two of you
slide as one block, you do not turn to face each other.

### Restrictions

From a Two-Faced Line the manual says the direction **must be toward the center of the Line**. A
"Veer Left" called the wrong way — away from the centre — is not this call. Callers choose the
direction that matches where the dancers already are and where the next call needs them.

### Common faults

| What happens | Why | The fix |
|---|---|---|
| You turn to face your partner | Treating it like a step to a wave | Veer keeps you side by side, both facing out |
| You turn in place instead of moving | Stopping first, then turning | Keep walking; the direction is added to the forward step |
| The couple separates | Each dancer stepped a different way | Move as a unit on the joined hands |
| You veer away from the centre of the line | Wrong direction chosen for the start formation | From a two-faced line, the named direction always points toward the middle |

### Where you meet it

Veer Left and Veer Right are taught in unit M17, in [Class 5](../05-course/class-05.md) of
[the course](../05-course/course-overview.md). They are short connective calls: callers use them to
turn facing couples into two-faced lines so the next call has the shape it needs.

### Related calls

- **[Lead Right / Lead Left](m16-lead-right-lead-left.md)** — the quarter-turn version, ending
  facing a couple.
- **[Bend the Line](m20-bend-the-line.md)** — the other way to change a line's shape.
- **[Couples Circulate](m19-circulate-family.md)** — the call that most often follows a Veer.
- **[Wheel and Deal](m26-wheel-and-deal.md)** — the usual way back out of a two-faced line.

### Also called / formerly

You will hear **"Veer Left"** and **"Veer Right"**; the manual's index also lists the pair as **"Veer
Left / Right"**. When callers use them as a pair — *"Veer Left, Veer Right"* — the dancers often feel
a ricochet as the direction reverses.
