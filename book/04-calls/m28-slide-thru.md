# M28 — Slide Thru

> **Teaching unit M28** of the CALLERLAB (New) Mainstream Suggested Teaching Order, effective
> 1 September 2026. Prerequisites: [Facing Dancers](../02-language/formations.md#facing-dancers),
> [Passing Rule](../03-rules/passing-rule.md), [Couple handhold](../02-language/hands-and-holds.md).

## Slide Thru

### At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Starting formation** | [Facing Dancers](../02-language/formations.md#facing-dancers) |
| **Ending formation** | [Couple](../02-language/formations.md#couple) — or a mini-wave if the two dancers are the same role |
| **Timing** | 4 beats |
| **Unit** | M28 of 50 |

### Animation

▶ **Play this call** — pause, step and scrub it: [Watching the animations](../08-reference/watching-the-animations.md).

### Dance action

> "In a single smooth motion, Pass Thru and Men Face Right, Women Face Left."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

The manual gives two more notes worth having: the turn "can be blended with the Pass Thru to finish
with a sliding or dodging motion", and the [Ocean Wave Rule](../03-rules/ocean-wave-rule.md) applies —
so Slide Thru can also start from a [mini-wave](../02-language/formations.md#waves).

### What you do

You are standing face to face with another dancer, a step or so apart.

1. **Walk forward.** Go straight ahead and pass the other dancer using the ordinary
   [Passing Rule](../03-rules/passing-rule.md) — right shoulders.
2. **Turn a quarter turn as you arrive** beside them, and stop.
   - **Boys turn to face right.**
   - **Girls turn to face left.**
3. **Stop there.** You are now side by side, both facing the same way — a
   [couple](../02-language/formations.md#couple) — exactly as if you had done a
   [Star Thru](m18-star-thru.md).

There is no hand action in the pass itself; the whole call is one smooth walk and a turn. The manual
calls it "a single smooth motion", and that is the point: do not pass, stop, then turn. The turn is
part of the step that takes you past.

If the dancer facing you is the same role as you — two Boys or two Girls — the ending is a
[mini-wave](../02-language/formations.md#waves) rather than a couple: two Boys finish in a right-hand
mini-wave, two Girls in a left-hand mini-wave.

### Timing

**4 beats** — two bars of music, the same as [Star Thru](m18-star-thru.md) and
[Pass Thru](m07-pass-thru.md) plus a turn. Nothing is rushed, but nothing is lingered over either;
the caller will normally be talking again as you arrive.

### Styling

> "Arms in natural dance position with skirt work optional for the women. Hands should be rejoined in
> the appropriate position (Couple or Hands Up handhold) for the next call."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

Because there is no handhold during the call, your arms simply stay in natural dance position. The
only styling decision is the one at the end: take an ordinary
[couple handhold](../02-language/hands-and-holds.md) if you finished as a couple, or a hands-up
mini-wave handhold if you are in a mini-wave — and do it before the next call starts.

### Common faults

| What happens | Why | The fix |
|---|---|---|
| You finish facing the other dancer again | You turned back instead of on through | Turn a quarter turn **past** them, so you end up side by side |
| You bump shoulders | Turning too early, before you are clear | Walk past first. The turn comes at the end of the step |
| You finish a full step too far apart | Walking too far past before turning | Turn *as* you arrive beside them |
| You turn the same way as the dancer facing you | Turning by habit rather than by role | Boys turn right, Girls turn left — you are aiming at the same direction as each other |

### Where you meet it

Slide Thru arrives in [Class 7](../05-course/class-07.md), with [Dive Thru](m29-dive-thru.md) and
[Box the Gnat](m30-box-the-gnat.md). It is a favourite of callers because it is short, it produces a
couple facing any way the caller wants, and it fits neatly into the middle of a singing call.

### Related calls

- **[Star Thru](m18-star-thru.md)** — the same destination, reached with an arch instead of a pass.
- **[Pass Thru](m07-pass-thru.md)** — the first half of Slide Thru, on its own.
- **[Box the Gnat](m30-box-the-gnat.md)** — pass and turn, but with hands joined throughout.
- **[Square Thru](m21-square-thru.md)** — the four-handed relative.

### Also called / formerly

Slide Thru has always been called Slide Thru; you will also hear **"Slide Through"**, which is the
same call. Older material says Men and Women where this book, following the 2026 programme, says Boys
and Girls.
