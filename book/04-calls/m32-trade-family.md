# M32 — Trade Family

> **Teaching unit M32** of the CALLERLAB (New) Mainstream Suggested Teaching Order, effective
> 1 September 2026. Prerequisites: [Ocean Wave](../02-language/formations.md#waves),
> [Facing Couples](../02-language/formations.md#facing-couples), [Two-Faced Lines](../02-language/formations.md#lines).

A **Trade** is two things swapping places by walking forward in a semicircle. The family has four
members: couples trading, named dancers trading, dancers still holding hands trading, and partners
trading. Learn the semicircle once and all four come easily.

## a. Couples Trade

### At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Starting formation** | [Two-Faced Line](../02-language/formations.md#lines) or [One-Faced Line](../02-language/formations.md#lines) — also non-adjacent couples |
| **Ending formation** | The same formation you started in |
| **Timing** | 6 beats from a Two-Faced Line; 8 beats from a One-Faced Line |
| **Unit** | M32a of 50 |

### Animation

▶ **Play this call** — pause, step and scrub it: [Watching the animations](../08-reference/watching-the-animations.md).

### Dance action

> "Couples, each working as a unit, exchange places by walking forward in a semicircle. If the
> Couples start facing in the same direction, the rightmost dancers in each Couple pass right
> shoulders."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

### What you do

1. **Stay in your couple handhold.** You move as a two-person unit, not as individuals.
2. **Walk forward in a semicircle** — a half circle — into the spot the other couple is standing in.
3. **If the two couples are facing the same way**, the dancer on the right of each couple passes
   right shoulder to right shoulder.

You finish in the same shape you started in, but the two couples have swapped ends. Everyone is now
facing the opposite direction from where they started.

### Timing

**6 beats** from a Two-Faced Line, where the couples are side by side. **8 beats** from a One-Faced
Line, where one couple is behind the other and the walk is longer. Allow extra beats if the couples
are not adjacent.

### Styling

> "Each Couple maintains their handhold. From a Two-Faced Line, Center dancers also maintain their
> handhold."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

That last sentence catches people out: if you are a Center dancer in a Two-Faced Line you are
already holding one hand with your partner *and* one hand with the dancer on the other side of the
line. Keep both. The inner handhold flows round the semicircle with you.

### Restrictions

The manual states that two couples may Trade **only** when they are facing the same or opposite
directions, and that each couple **must** be directly to the right or left of the other. They do not
need to be adjacent — a gap is fine. If there are four couples in a row, each outside couple trades
with the nearby inside couple.

### Common faults

| What happens | Why | The fix |
|---|---|---|
| You let go of your partner halfway round | Trying to walk as an individual | The handhold stays made for the whole semicircle |
| Center dancers drop the inner handhold | Not realising the other hand is also held | From a Two-Faced Line, keep both hands joined |
| You walk a full circle instead of a half | Over-turning | Semicircle: you stop as soon as you reach the other couple's spot |
| You finish facing the same way you started | Turning back at the end | The semicircle leaves you facing the opposite direction — do not correct it |

### Where you meet it

Couples Trade is the standard way to move two couples past each other without breaking the line. It
appears after [Wheel and Deal](m26-wheel-and-deal.md), after [Box Circulate](m19-circulate-family.md),
and anywhere a caller wants to change which dancers are the Centers. See the
[course overview](../05-course/course-overview.md) for where this unit sits.

### Related calls

- **[(Named Dancers) Trade](#b-named-dancers-trade)** — the same action for individual dancers.
- **[Partner Trade](#d-partner-trade)** — the same action for you and your partner.
- **[Wheel and Deal](m26-wheel-and-deal.md)** — the other way to move a couple as a unit.

### Also called / formerly

Callers add helping words such as **"Center 4, Couples Trade"**, **"Outside 4, Couples Trade"** and
**"Couples Trade by the Right"**. The extra words say which couples or which shoulder, not a
different call.

## b. (Named Dancers) Trade

### At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Starting formation** | [Mini-Wave](../02-language/formations.md#waves), [Couple](../02-language/formations.md#couple), or non-adjacent dancers |
| **Ending formation** | The same formation you started in |
| **Timing** | 3 beats from a Mini-Wave; 4 beats from a Couple |
| **Unit** | M32b of 50 |

### Animation

▶ **Play this call** — pause, step and scrub it: [Watching the animations](../08-reference/watching-the-animations.md).

### Dance action

> "Two dancers exchange places by walking forward in a semicircle, passing right shoulders if they
> start facing the same direction. Dancers end facing the opposite direction from their original
> facing directions."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

### What you do

1. **Wait for the name.** The caller says *who* trades — "Boys Trade", "Girls Trade", "Centers
   Trade", "Ends Trade".
2. **If you are named, walk forward in a semicircle** into the spot of the other named dancer beside
   you.
3. **If you and that dancer are facing the same way**, pass right shoulders as you go.
4. **If you are not named, stand still.** You are not part of the call.

You finish where the other dancer started, facing the opposite way. Everyone else has not moved.

### Timing

**3 beats** from a Mini-Wave — a quick half circle. **4 beats** from a Couple, where the handhold has
to release and re-form. Allow more if the named dancers are not adjacent.

### Styling

> "From a Mini-Wave, dancers maintain their handhold."

> "From a Couple, dancers swing their joined hands backward slightly as they release their handhold.
> This gets them turning in the correct direction as they start moving forward."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

The little backward swing on the handhold is a starting handle. Do it and the semicircle turns the
right way by itself.

### Restrictions

The manual states that two dancers may Trade **only** when they are facing the same or opposite
directions, and each dancer **must** be directly to the right or left of the other. They need not be
adjacent. When the dancers are in a long line, the caller **must** say which dancers are meant — for
example, "Each Side, Boys Trade".

### Common faults

| What happens | Why | The fix |
|---|---|---|
| Dancers who were not named move too | Everybody assuming they are active | Only the named dancers move; the rest stand still |
| You turn the wrong way out of the semicircle | Skipping the backward hand swing | Swing the joined hands back as you let go — it points you the right way |
| You end up facing the same direction | Adding a turn | End facing the **opposite** way; that is the whole point of the call |
| You trade with the wrong dancer | Picking a convenient neighbour | Trade with the named dancer directly to your right or left |

### Where you meet it

This is the everyday form of Trade. "Boys Trade" and "Girls Trade" appear constantly in singing
calls, almost always from an Ocean Wave. Trade with no name at all means **everyone** Trade. See the
[course overview](../05-course/course-overview.md) for where this unit sits.

### Related calls

- **Couples Trade** — the same action for whole couples. See [M32a](#a-couples-trade).
- **Right/Left Hand Trade** — the same action without releasing hands. See [M32c](#c-rightleft-hand-trade).
- **Swing Thru** — the common call that follows a wave Trade. See [M33](m33-swing-thru.md).

### Also called / formerly

You will hear **"Centers Trade"**, **"Ends Trade"**, **"Boys Trade"**, **"Girls Trade"**, **"Center
6, Trade"** and plain **"Trade"**. Plain "Trade" means everyone trades.

## c. Right/Left Hand Trade

### At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Starting formation** | [Mini-Wave](../02-language/formations.md#waves) |
| **Ending formation** | [Mini-Wave](../02-language/formations.md#waves) |
| **Timing** | 3 beats |
| **Unit** | M32c of 50 |

### Animation

***No animation yet.*** *There is no animation of this call.*

### Dance action

> "Two dancers exchange places by walking forward in a semicircle while maintaining their hand
> hold. All active dancers end facing the opposite direction from their original facing directions."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

### What you do

1. **Keep hold of the hand you and your neighbour already have joined** — right hand or left hand,
   whichever it is.
2. **Both walk forward in a semicircle** past each other, still holding on.
3. **Stop in the other dancer's spot**, facing the opposite way.

It is exactly a [named-dancer Trade](#b-named-dancers-trade), except that you never let go. The
curved path and the joined hands pass each other without a shoulder pass.

### Timing

**3 beats** — the same as any Mini-Wave Trade. The handhold never breaks, so nothing slows you down.

### Styling

> "Dancers maintain their handhold."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

Keep the joined hands at a comfortable height between you and bend the elbows as you turn. The
handhold becomes the hinge of the semicircle.

### Restrictions

The manual states that **"Trade by the Right"** (or Left) means each pair who trade are in a
**Right-Hand** (or Left-Hand) [Mini-Wave](../02-language/formations.md#waves) — so the hand being held
tells you which version the caller means. In some formations helping words are **necessary** to
identify the pairs, for example "Trade by the Right" from an Alamo Ring.

### Common faults

| What happens | Why | The fix |
|---|---|---|
| You let go of the hand and Trade normally | Old habit from (Named Dancers) Trade | This call is defined by the handhold staying made |
| You mix it up with [Trade By](m39-trade-by.md) | The two names sound almost the same | Trade By is an eight-dancer call with Centers and Outsides; this one is a two-dancer call |
| You end facing your partner | Half-turning | The semicircle finishes with you facing the opposite direction |
| You hold the wrong hand | Not listening for "by the Right" / "by the Left" | The side named is the hand you should already be holding |

### Where you meet it

Right/Left Hand Trade is most useful from an Alamo Ring or an Inverted Line, where a caller needs to
say exactly which dancers and which hand are meant. It is much rarer than the plain
[(Named Dancers) Trade](#b-named-dancers-trade). See the
[course overview](../05-course/course-overview.md) for where this unit sits.

### Related calls

- **(Named Dancers) Trade** — the same thing with the handhold released. See [M32b](#b-named-dancers-trade).
- **Trade By** — an eight-dancer call with a confusingly similar name. See [M39](m39-trade-by.md).
- **Alamo Style** — a formation where this call needs helping words. See [M31a](m31-ocean-wave-family.md).

### Also called / formerly

The manual notes that **"Trade with the right hand"** is sometimes used instead of **"Trade by the
Right"**, mainly because outside English-speaking countries dancers confuse "Trade by the Right" with
"Trade By". If you hear either phrase, listen for the extra "hand".

## d. Partner Trade

### At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Starting formation** | [Couple](../02-language/formations.md#couple) **only** |
| **Ending formation** | [Couple](../02-language/formations.md#couple), facing the opposite direction |
| **Timing** | 4 beats |
| **Unit** | M32d of 50 |

### Animation

▶ **Play this call** — pause, step and scrub it: [Watching the animations](../08-reference/watching-the-animations.md).

### Dance action

> "Two dancers exchange places by walking forward in a semicircle, passing right shoulders as they
> do so."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

### What you do

1. **Let go of the couple handhold**, swinging your joined hands backward a little as you release.
2. **Both walk forward in a semicircle**, passing **right** shoulders with your partner.
3. **Stop on your partner's spot**, still facing the same way you were going but now on the other
   side.

You finish as a couple facing exactly the opposite direction from where you started, with your
partner on your other side. Take the [couple handhold](../02-language/hands-and-holds.md) again.

### Timing

**4 beats** — a comfortable walking half circle. It is the same shape and timing as
[(Named Dancers) Trade](#b-named-dancers-trade) from a couple.

### Styling

> "Dancers swing their joined hands backward slightly as they release their handhold. This gets them
> turning in the correct direction as they start moving forward."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

The backward swing is small — a few inches — but it is what turns the pair outward instead of
inward. Without it dancers tend to bump.

### Restrictions

The manual is firm here in three places. Partner Trade starts from a **Couple only**. It is
**improper** when the two dancers are not adjacent. And in 2017 the Mainstream Committee voted to
**restrict** Partner Trade to start from a Couple only — so if you are beside someone you are not
partnered with, the caller will use [Trade](#b-named-dancers-trade) instead.

### Common faults

| What happens | Why | The fix |
|---|---|---|
| You pass left shoulders and get tangled | Turning the wrong way | Always pass **right** shoulders |
| You end up facing each other | Walking a full circle | Stop at the opposite spot; you finish back-to-back with where you started |
| You drift a long way out of the set | Walking a wide, lazy arc | The semicircle is tight — your partner's spot is only one position away |
| You stay joined and drag your partner round | Forgetting to release | Let go, swing the hands back, then walk |

### Where you meet it

Partner Trade is a short, tidy way to turn a couple around, and it is the Outsides' part of
[Trade By](m39-trade-by.md). You also meet it after a Double Pass Thru, as "Leaders Partner Trade".
See the [course overview](../05-course/course-overview.md) for where this unit sits.

### Related calls

- **Couples Trade** — the same action for two couples as units. See [M32a](#a-couples-trade).
- **(Named Dancers) Trade** — the same action for named individuals. See [M32b](#b-named-dancers-trade).
- **Trade By** — the eight-dancer call that uses Partner Trade. See [M39](m39-trade-by.md).
- **California Twirl** — another way to turn a couple around, using an arch. See [M27](m27-california-twirl.md).

### Also called / formerly

The call is normally given as **"Partner Trade"**. Helping words name the dancers rather than change
the call, as in **"Leaders Partner Trade"** or **"Boys Partner Trade"**.
