### Dance action

> "The runner and non-runner exchange places: the runner walks forward in a semicircle into the
> non-runner's spot (ending with the opposite facing direction), while the non-runner moves into the
> runner's spot without changing facing direction."

> "The cross-runners walk forward in a semi-circle into the spot vacated by the farther non-runner
> (ending with the opposite facing direction). While doing this, each cross-runner will cross the
> center of the four-dancer formation. Simultaneously each non-runner moves into the spot of the
> closer cross-runner without changing facing direction."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

### What you do

The caller names who runs — "Boys Run", "Centers Run", "Girls Cross Run". The named dancers are the
**runners**; everybody else in the group is a **non-runner**.

**For Run:**

1. **Find the non-runner beside you** — the dancer to your right or your left, whichever the caller
   named. You are now a working pair.
2. **If you are the runner**, walk forward in a semicircle around that dancer and stop in their spot,
   facing the opposite way from how you started.
3. **If you are the non-runner**, step straight sideways into the runner's spot. Do **not** turn
   around — you keep facing exactly the way you were.

**For Cross Run**, the same idea across a line of four:

1. **The cross-runners** are the two Centers or the two Ends, as the caller names. The other two are
   non-runners.
2. **Each cross-runner walks a semicircle across the middle of the line**, past the far non-runner,
   and stops in that dancer's spot, facing the opposite way.
3. **Each non-runner moves one spot inwards** — an End moves to the nearer Center spot, a Center
   moves to the nearer End spot — without changing facing direction.

### Timing

**Run is 4 beats** — one smooth half circle. **Cross Run is 6 beats** because the runner travels
further, all the way across the line.

### Styling

> "Hands should blend into the handhold required for the ending formation (either Couple or
> Mini-Wave)."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

Let go at the start if you need to, then take the right handhold as you land. Do not try to keep a
handhold through the semicircle — the path is too long and you will drag your neighbour.

### Restrictions

The manual states that for Cross Run the cross-runners **must** both be Centers or both be Ends. It
also records that **at Mainstream the runner and non-runner will almost always begin as a Mini-Wave
or a Couple** — other arrangements need a workshop first.

Two further points are worth knowing. Direction words identify which side the non-runner is on; they
are often optional ("Swing Thru, Boys Run Right") but are **required** to avoid ambiguity, for
example from an Alamo Ring or a Circle, or in "Center Girls Run Left". And "Boys Run" is **improper**
in a situation where it is unclear whether the Girls should do the non-runner's part or not move at
all; the caller uses "Do Your Part" instead.

### Common faults

| What happens | Why | The fix |
|---|---|---|
| The non-runner turns to face the other way | Copying the runner | The non-runner's facing direction does **not** change — only the runner's does |
| You run past the dancer instead of around them | Walking straight | The runner walks a semicircle into the adjacent spot |
| Cross Run dancers bump in the middle | Both crossing on the same side | Cross-runners pass in the center; each crosses to the far non-runner's spot |
| You run in the wrong direction | Missing the direction word | Listen for "Run Right" / "Run Left" — it says which side the non-runner is on |

### Where you meet it

Run turns up constantly after [Swing Thru](m33-swing-thru.md), after
[Pass the Ocean](m35-pass-the-ocean.md) and anywhere a caller wants to change which dancers are in
the Center of a wave. "Boys Run" and "Girls Run" are among the most used calls in the program. See
the [course overview](../05-course/course-overview.md) for where this unit sits.

### Also called / formerly

You will hear **"Boys Run"**, **"Girls Run"**, **"Centers Run"**, **"Ends Run"**, **"Leaders Run"**,
**"Women Run Right"** and **"Girls Cross Run"**, **"Centers Cross Run"**, **"Ends Cross Run"**. The
names say who runs; the direction words say which side the non-runner is on.
