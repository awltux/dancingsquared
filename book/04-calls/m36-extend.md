# M36 — Extend

> **Teaching unit M36** of the CALLERLAB (New) Mainstream Suggested Teaching Order, effective
> 1 September 2026. Prerequisites: [Tag formations](../02-language/formations.md#tag-formations),
> [Ocean Wave](../02-language/formations.md#waves), [Columns](../02-language/formations.md#columns).

## Extend

### At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Starting formation** | One of the first four [tagging formations](../02-language/formations.md#tag-formations): Double Pass Thru, 1/4 Tag, Ocean Waves, or 3/4 Tag |
| **Ending formation** | The next tagging formation along: 1/4 Tag, Ocean Waves, 3/4 Tag, or Completed Double Pass Thru |
| **Timing** | 2 beats |
| **Unit** | M36 of 50 |

### Animation

▶ **Play this call** — pause, step and scrub it: [Watching the animations](../08-reference/watching-the-animations.md).

### Dance action

> "Walk forward from one tagging formation to the next. Where possible, handedness is maintained,
> as described in the comments below."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

### What you do

Extend is a single step forward along a ladder of five formations. The ladder, in order, is:

1. **Double Pass Thru** (also called Zero Tag)
2. **1/4 Tag**
3. **Ocean Waves** (also called 1/2 Tag)
4. **3/4 Tag**
5. **Completed Double Pass Thru** (also called Full Tag)

Extend moves you exactly one rung up that ladder.

1. **Find where you are on the ladder.** Almost always that means the wave at position 3, or the
   1/4 Tag at position 2.
2. **Check your hands.** If you are holding a right hand now, you will still be holding a right hand
   after the Extend.
3. **Walk forward one step**, straight ahead, into the next formation.
4. **Take the handhold for the new formation** — a [couple handhold](../02-language/hands-and-holds.md)
   or an Ocean Wave hand position.

Nothing else changes. Extend does not turn anybody around.

### Timing

**2 beats** — one step forward and settle. It is the shortest call in this part of the program, which
is why callers often run it straight into the next call.

### Styling

> "All dancers should have their hands in ready dancing position for the formation resulting from
> the call (Couple handhold or Ocean Wave hand position)."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

Have your hands ready *before* you arrive, so the next call can start immediately. Extend is usually
the filling between two calls, not a thing in itself.

### Restrictions

The manual lists several limits. **Extend is improper from Facing Lines or a Tidal Wave**; from those
formations callers should use "Step to a Wave", "Step Thru" or "Pass Thru" instead. It is also
**improper to use Extend to change handedness** — "Heads Pass the Ocean, Extend to Left-Hand Waves"
is wrong.

From a 1/4 Tag, the phrases **"Centers Extend"** and **"Centers Extend to the Outsides"** should not
be used, because they wrongly suggest that only the Centers move. Everyone is active and everyone
moves. Asking **only some** dancers to Extend — for example "Heads Pass the Ocean, Just the Boys
Extend" — is classed as a gimmick.

### Common faults

| What happens | Why | The fix |
|---|---|---|
| Half the square does not move | Assuming only the Centers are active | Everyone moves; step forward together |
| You end up in a left-hand wave | Adding a hand change | Extend never changes handedness — right stays right |
| You take two steps and overshoot | Treating it as a walk across the square | One step to the very next formation, then stop |
| You arrive without your hands ready | Leaving the handhold until later | Be in the new handhold as you land |

### Where you meet it

Extend is a connector. You will hear it after "Heads Pass the Ocean" (to reach the wave),
after [Pass the Ocean](m35-pass-the-ocean.md) from a 1/4 Tag, and before calls such as
[Swing Thru](m33-swing-thru.md) that need a full wave. It was originally named **"Extend the Tag"**.
See the [course overview](../05-course/course-overview.md) for where this unit sits.

### Also called / formerly

The call was originally named **"Extend the Tag"**. In 1988 the name was shortened to **"Extend"**
without any change to the dance action. When the starting formation is left-handed, callers often
help by saying **"Extend to Left-Hand Waves"**.
