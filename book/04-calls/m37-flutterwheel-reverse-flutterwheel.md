# M37 — Flutterwheel / Reverse Flutterwheel

> **Teaching unit M37** of the CALLERLAB (New) Mainstream Suggested Teaching Order, effective
> 1 September 2026. Prerequisites: [Facing Couples](../02-language/formations.md#facing-couples),
> [Arm Turn](m04-arm-turn-family.md), [Couple handhold](../02-language/hands-and-holds.md).

## Flutterwheel / Reverse Flutterwheel

### At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Starting formation** | [Facing Couples](../02-language/formations.md#facing-couples) |
| **Ending formation** | [Facing Couples](../02-language/formations.md#facing-couples) |
| **Timing** | 8 beats |
| **Unit** | M37 of 50 |

### Animation

▶ **Play this call** — pause, step and scrub it: [Watching the animations](../08-reference/watching-the-animations.md).

### Dance action

> "The right-side dancers leave their current Partner and Right Arm Turn with each other a full
> turn, each ending where they started. Halfway through this motion, they take a Couple handhold
> with the other dancer (that is, the one they were originally facing directly), and the second half
> of the Arm Turn is completed with each new Couple working as a unit."

> "Use the same dance action as for Flutterwheel, except that the left-side dancers Left Arm Turn."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

### What you do

Start in facing couples — you facing your partner, with another couple facing you.

1. **Step out of your couple handhold.** In a normal couple one of you is on the dancer's right side
   and one on the left.
2. **If you are the right-side dancer**, turn to face the other right-side dancer across from you and
   take a **right forearm** hold.
3. **The two of you Arm Turn a full turn**, each finishing where you started.
4. **Halfway round**, reach out and take a
   [couple handhold](../02-language/hands-and-holds.md) with the dancer you were originally facing
   directly — that is, your new partner.
5. **Finish the second half of the arm turn** as a couple, moving as one unit.

You finish in facing couples again, but with new partners: everyone has swapped.

For **Reverse Flutterwheel**, it is exactly the same except that the **left-side** dancers arm turn
by the **left**.

### Timing

**8 beats** — a full turn at a walking pace. The manual notes that in a Square Thru style the same
figure works out at about twelve steps.

### Styling

> "Each dancer who is on the outside blends smoothly into a new Couple by turning slightly to become
> adjacent to the approaching dancer and reaching out to create a Couple handhold. Some dancers
> begin moving forward before hand contact is made and then walk slightly ahead of their new Partner
> so that the overall motion is smoother."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

The reach for the new partner starts *before* the arm turn ends. Waiting until you have stopped makes
the call jerky. When the outside dancers are women they may use skirt work with the free hand.

### Common faults

| What happens | Why | The fix |
|---|---|---|
| You arm turn your own partner | Not stepping out of the couple first | The arm turn is with the dancer on the **other** couple's same side |
| You never find the new handhold | Waiting until the turn is over | Reach out at the halfway point, not at the end |
| The new couples are not facing each other | Taking the wrong hand or turning the wrong way | Flutterwheel is a **right** arm turn; the new couple holds hands and faces the other couple |
| Reverse Flutterwheel goes the wrong way | Using the right-side dancers | In the Reverse, the **left**-side dancers Left Arm Turn |

### Where you meet it

Flutterwheel turns up in singing calls as a partner-changing figure, often followed by
[Sweep a Quarter](m38-sweep-a-quarter.md) or by a Promenade — "Reverse Flutterwheel, Promenade Home"
is a classic ending. Callers also use **"Ladies Lead, Flutterwheel"** as a helping phrase. See the
[course overview](../05-course/course-overview.md) for where this unit sits.

### Related calls

### Also called / formerly

**"Reverse the Flutter"** is a common short form of Reverse Flutterwheel. In **"Ladies Lead,
Flutterwheel"** the extra words do not change the dance action; they are added to be helpful. From a
Static Square, **"All Four Ladies Lead, Flutterwheel"** has all four Ladies make a Right-Hand Star,
go halfway, then take a couple handhold with the Gent there and continue around as new couples.
