# M38 — Sweep a Quarter

> **Teaching unit M38** of the CALLERLAB (New) Mainstream Suggested Teaching Order, effective
> 1 September 2026. Prerequisites: [Facing Couples](../02-language/formations.md#facing-couples),
> [Circle Left/Right](m01-circle-family.md), [Flutterwheel](m37-flutterwheel-reverse-flutterwheel.md).

## Sweep a Quarter

### At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Starting formation** | [Facing Couples](../02-language/formations.md#facing-couples) **only** |
| **Ending formation** | [Facing Couples](../02-language/formations.md#facing-couples) |
| **Timing** | 2 beats |
| **Unit** | M38 of 50 |

### Animation

▶ **Play this call** — pause, step and scrub it: [Watching the animations](../08-reference/watching-the-animations.md).

### Dance action

> "Circle Right or Left 1/4 (page 23), except that each Couple does not join hands with the Couple
> they are facing. The circling direction continues the motion that completed the previous call."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

### What you do

Sweep a Quarter is a **suffix call**: it has no meaning on its own and always follows another call.

1. **Finish the call that came before.** As you finish, notice which way you are already moving
   around the center of the group — clockwise or counter-clockwise.
2. **Keep that same direction.** Do not reverse.
3. **Both couples sweep one quarter turn** (90 degrees) around the center, **without joining hands
   with the other couple**. You keep only your own [couple handhold](../02-language/hands-and-holds.md).
4. **Stop after a quarter turn.** You are once again two couples facing each other.

It is a Circle Left or Circle Right of one quarter — but with the circle made by couples, not by
joined hands all the way round.

### Timing

**2 beats** — one quick quarter turn. It is usually attached to the end of the previous call with no
gap at all.

### Styling

> "Dancers use a Couple handhold."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

Hold your partner and move together. There is no eight-hand circle, so all the turning comes from you
and your partner's two-handed grip.

### Restrictions

The manual gives the starting formation as **[Facing Couples](../02-language/formations.md#facing-couples) only**,
and it lists several places where the call is **improper**:

- After **Chain Down the Line** — the dancers who started in the center are not turning around the
  center of the new facing couples.
- After **Bend the Line**, **Wheel Around**, **Cast Off 3/4**, and similar calls, for the same reason.
- After **Ferris Wheel** (or Wheel and Deal from Lines Facing Out), plain "Sweep a Quarter" is
  improper, but **"Centers Sweep a Quarter"** is proper.

There is one exception made because of years of use: from a wave, **"Recycle, Sweep a Quarter"** is
proper, even though some dancers finish Recycle with a turn in place.

### Common faults

| What happens | Why | The fix |
|---|---|---|
| You turn the wrong way | Guessing instead of reading the previous call | The sweep continues the direction the last call left you moving |
| You reach for the other couple's hands | Thinking of it as a circle | No hands with the facing couple — only your partner |
| You sweep a half turn | Over-turning | A quarter turn is 90 degrees; you stop facing the other couple |
| You take a pause before starting | Treating it as a separate call | The sweep picks up the motion of the previous call with no break |

### Where you meet it

Sweep a Quarter is bolted onto the end of calls that leave you in facing couples — most often
[Flutterwheel](m37-flutterwheel-reverse-flutterwheel.md), as in "Flutterwheel and Sweep a Quarter".
It also appears as "Centers Sweep a Quarter" after a Ferris Wheel. See the
[course overview](../05-course/course-overview.md) for where this unit sits.

### Related calls

- **[Flutterwheel](m37-flutterwheel-reverse-flutterwheel.md)** — the call it most often follows.
- **[Circle Left/Right](m01-circle-family.md)** — the plain quarter circle it is built from.
- **[Ferris Wheel](m40-ferris-wheel.md)** — after it, only "Centers Sweep a Quarter" is proper.

### Also called / formerly

You will hear **"Sweep One Quarter"** and **"Sweep a Quarter"**. For more than one quarter, callers
prefer **"Sweep a Quarter, Sweep another Quarter"**, **"Sweep Two Quarters"** or **"Sweep One
Half"**; "Sweep a Quarter Twice" is uncommon. The manual notes care is needed with groups whose
first language is not English.
