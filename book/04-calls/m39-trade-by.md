# M39 — Trade By

> **Teaching unit M39** of the CALLERLAB (New) Mainstream Suggested Teaching Order, effective
> 1 September 2026. Prerequisites: [Pass Thru](m07-pass-thru.md),
> [Partner Trade](m32-trade-family.md), [Ocean Wave Rule](../03-rules/ocean-wave-rule.md).

## Trade By

### At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Starting formation** | The Trade By formation — two facing couples in the center, two couples facing out on the ends |
| **Ending formation** | Eight Chain Thru formation |
| **Timing** | 4 beats |
| **Unit** | M39 of 50 |

### Animation

▶ **Play this call** — pause, step and scrub it: [Watching the animations](../08-reference/watching-the-animations.md).

### Dance action

> "Centers Pass Thru while Outsides, who must be Couples facing out, Partner Trade"

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

### What you do

The square is arranged with two couples facing each other in the middle and one couple at each end
facing out. Everyone moves at the same time, but the two halves of the square do different jobs.

**If you are a Center dancer:**

1. **Pass Thru.** Walk forward past the dancer facing you, passing right shoulders, and stop.

**If you are an Outside dancer:**

2. **Stay in your couple handhold** and **Partner Trade** — walk forward in a semicircle with your
   partner, passing right shoulders, and finish on your partner's spot, facing the opposite way.

You finish in an **Eight Chain Thru formation**: the former Centers are now back-to-back on the
outside, and the former Outsides are facing in as couples on the ends. The next call is very often
[Right and Left Thru](m10-right-and-left-thru.md) or an Eight Chain.

### Timing

**4 beats** — the Centers' Pass Thru and the Outsides' Partner Trade are both four beats, so the
whole square finishes together.

### Styling

> "Same as for Pass Thru (page 44) and Partner Trade (page 63)"

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

In plain words: Centers pass with a natural arm position and the Man's right shoulder slightly
forward; Outsides swing their joined hands backward slightly as they release the couple handhold and
turn the correct way.

### Restrictions

The manual states that the Outsides **must be Couples facing out** — that is what makes the Trade By
formation. It also records that the **[Ocean Wave Rule](../03-rules/ocean-wave-rule.md) applies to
the Center dancers**, for example from a 3/4 Tag formation, so the Centers may begin in a wave rather
than strictly facing each other.

### Common faults

| What happens | Why | The fix |
|---|---|---|
| The Centers and Outsides collide | Nobody agreed who is active | Everyone moves, but Centers pass through the middle and Outsides trade on the ends |
| An Outside dancer Turns alone | Forgetting the couple handhold | Outsides do a **Partner** Trade — you and your partner move as a pair |
| The Centers end facing their partner | Turning after the Pass Thru | Centers do a plain Pass Thru and finish back-to-back |
| The square does not balance | One half moving faster than the other | Both halves are four beats; adjust your speed to finish together |

### Where you meet it

Trade By is how you turn the Trade By formation into an Eight Chain Thru formation, ready for a
Right and Left Thru or a Square Thru. It is one of the standard ways to bring the square back to an
Eight Chain Thru. See the [course overview](../05-course/course-overview.md) for where this unit
sits.

### Related calls

- **[Partner Trade](m32-trade-family.md)** — the Outsides' half of the call.
- **[Pass Thru](m07-pass-thru.md)** — the Centers' half of the call.
- **[Right and Left Thru](m10-right-and-left-thru.md)** — the usual next call from an Eight Chain
  Thru.
- **[Right/Left Hand Trade](m32-trade-family.md)** — a two-dancer call with a confusingly similar
  name.

### Also called / formerly

The call is always called **"Trade By"**. Do not confuse it with **"Trade by the Right"**, which is
the two-dancer [Right/Left Hand Trade](m32-trade-family.md) — the manual warns that callers outside
English-speaking countries may need to say "Trade with the right hand" instead.
