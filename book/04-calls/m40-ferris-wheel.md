# M40 — Ferris Wheel

> **Teaching unit M40** of the CALLERLAB (New) Mainstream Suggested Teaching Order, effective
> 1 September 2026. Prerequisites: [Two-Faced Lines](../02-language/formations.md#lines),
> [Wheel and Deal](m26-wheel-and-deal.md), [Double Pass Thru](m24-double-pass-thru.md).

## Ferris Wheel

### At a glance

| | |

| **Starting formation** | [Two-Faced Lines](../02-language/formations.md#lines) — right-handed, strictly |
| **Ending formation** | [Double Pass Thru](../02-language/formations.md#double-pass-thru) |

| **Unit** | M40 of 50 |

### Dance action

> "Each Couple Steps Forward. Out-facing Couples do their part, Wheel and Deal. In-facing Couples
> form a momentary Two-Faced Line in the center, and without stopping Wheel and Deal."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

### What you do

You start in Two-Faced Lines: two couples with their backs to the middle of the square, facing out,
and two couples facing in towards them.

**Everyone starts by stepping forward** — that part is the same for all four couples.

**If your couple is facing out:**

1. **Wheel and Deal** — the two of you, still holding your couple handhold, wheel around as a unit a
   half turn and finish facing the other way.

**If your couple is facing in:**

2. **Walk forward far enough to meet the other in-facing couple** in the center. The two of you form a
   momentary Two-Faced Line with them — a quick touch, not a stop.
3. **Without stopping, Wheel and Deal** as a couple from there.

You finish in a Double Pass Thru formation, ready to pass through and keep going.

### Timing

**6 beats** — the two halves of the square travel different distances, so the timing is a compromise.
The manual notes it works best if the **out-facing couples adjust their speed** so that everybody
finishes at the same moment.

### Styling

> "All dancers use Couple handholds. The Infacing Couples should walk forward enough that they could
> form a Two-Faced Line in the center before starting their Wheel and Deal. The dance action should
> be a forward and wheeling action, not a bending and sweeping action."

> "It is not necessary for the Couples to touch adjacent hands in the momentary Two-Faced Line, but
> some popular styling variations do involve touching or slapping hands."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

"Forward and wheeling" is the thing to remember. Dancers who bend at the waist and sweep their arms
around produce a slow, sagging Ferris Wheel. Dancers who step forward and then wheel produce a
smooth one.

### Restrictions

The manual is clear that the phrase **"Ferris Wheel and Deal" is improper** — Ferris Wheel already
contains a Wheel and Deal, so the extra words do not describe anything. It also records that the
**original starting formations** for Ferris Wheel (Facing Lines, 1/4 Line) **have never been part of
the CALLERLAB program**, so at Mainstream you should expect right-handed Two-Faced Lines.

One further application is legal but uncommon: from the formation "Two-Faced Lines plus the Couple
looking in Bend the Line", Ferris Wheel is proper, with each couple doing their part.

### Common faults

| What happens | Why | The fix |
|---|---|---|
| You bend over and sweep your arms round | Dancing it as a bend instead of a wheel | Step forward first, then wheel — a wheeling action, not a bending one |
| The in-facing couples stop in the middle | Waiting to form the line properly | The Two-Faced Line is momentary; wheel straight through it |
| The square finishes raggedly | The out-facing couples going too fast or too slow | Out-facing couples adjust their speed to finish at the same time |
| You end up back where you started | Wheeling a full turn | It is a half turn — you finish facing the opposite way, in a Double Pass Thru |

### Where you meet it

Ferris Wheel is the standard way to turn two-faced lines into a Double Pass Thru, and from there the
square usually rolls straight on into a [Double Pass Thru](m24-double-pass-thru.md) or a
[Wheel and Deal](m26-wheel-and-deal.md). It appears in singing calls and in figures such as
"Ferris Wheel, Centers Sweep a Quarter". See the
[course overview](../05-course/course-overview.md) for where this unit sits.

and Deal"**, which the manual marks improper.
