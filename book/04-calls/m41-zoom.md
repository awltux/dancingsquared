# M41 — Zoom

> **Teaching unit M41** of the CALLERLAB (New) Mainstream Suggested Teaching Order, effective
> 1 September 2026. Prerequisites: [Leaders and Trailers](../02-language/naming-the-dancers.md),
> [Box Circulate](m19-circulate-family.md).

## Zoom

### At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Starting formation** | A Tandem and a centre point — usually [Tandem Couples](../02-language/naming-the-dancers.md) or a [Box Circulate](../02-language/formations.md#box) |
| **Ending formation** | The same as the starting formation |
| **Timing** | 4 beats |
| **Unit** | M41 of 50 |

### Animation

▶ **Play this call** — pause, step and scrub it: [Watching the animations](../08-reference/watching-the-animations.md).

### Dance action

> "Lead dancer walks in a full circle, turning away from the center point, and ending up on the spot
> of the Trailing dancer. The Trailing dancer walks forward to take the spot of the Lead dancer."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

### What you do

1. **Find your centre point.** This is the middle of the four-dancer group you are part of — a box,
   or a pair of [Tandem](../02-language/naming-the-dancers.md) dancers.
2. **Know whether you are a Leader or a Trailer.** The Leader is the one facing out of the box; the
   Trailer is the one facing into it.
3. **If you are the Leader**, step slightly forward and then roll away from the centre point. Walk a
   full circle and stop on the spot your Trailer started from.
4. **If you are the Trailer**, walk straight forward and take the spot your Leader started from.

Nobody turns to face a new direction at the end: the Trailer's forward walk and the Leader's roll
put you both facing exactly the way you were at the start. Only the two of you have swapped places.

### Timing

**4 beats** — two bars of music at a walking pace. This is exactly the timing of one
[Circulate](m19-circulate-family.md), which is why Zoom slips easily into the same phrase.

The animation runs over six beats, so it is drawn a little longer than the manual
asks for.

### Styling

> "Lead dancers hold arms in natural dance position. For women, skirt work is optional. When the
> Trailing dancers form a Couple, they maintain a Couple handhold."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

> "It is important that the Lead dancers initiate the roll out movement with a slight forward motion
> to allow sufficient room for the Trailing dancers to step forward comfortably."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

The small forward step by the Leader is what makes the call work. Without it the Trailer has nowhere
to walk, and the two of you collide in the middle.

### Common faults

| What happens | Why | The fix |
|---|---|---|
| The Leader turns the wrong way and the pair ends up facing out | Turning *toward* the centre instead of away | The Leader always rolls **away** from the centre point |
| The pair ends up too close together | The Leader walks a half circle instead of a full one | It is a full circle — the Leader finishes on the Trailer's spot, not beside it |
| You both end up facing a new direction | Attempting an extra turn at the end | Do not turn at the end; the paths themselves leave you facing the same way |
| The Trailer bumps into the Leader halfway | The Leader did not step forward first | The Leader's small forward step opens the space the Trailer needs |

### Where you meet it

Zoom is one of the later units in the teaching order, and it is a common way to repair a box or a
tandem that has drifted out of shape. You will meet it most often phrased as **"Ends Zoom"**, called
to the two outside dancers of an [Ocean Wave](../02-language/formations.md#waves) or a Two-Faced Line. See
the [course overview](../05-course/course-overview.md) for where this unit sits.

### Related calls

- **[Circulate](m19-circulate-family.md)** — the same half-set action without the roll.
- **[Run / Cross Run](m34-run-cross-run.md)** — a half circle to the adjacent spot instead of a full
  one.
- **[Fold / Cross Fold](m48-fold-cross-fold.md)** — the other half-call built on a semicircle.
- **[Scoot Back](m47-scoot-back.md)** — works the same box but ends with an arm turn.

### Also called / formerly

Zoom has always been called **Zoom**. Some callers use the helping words **"Zoom, Leaders go back"**
to make it clear who is active, so do not be surprised to hear the extra phrase.
