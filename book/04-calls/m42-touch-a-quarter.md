# M42 — Touch a Quarter

> **Teaching unit M42** of the CALLERLAB (New) Mainstream Suggested Teaching Order, effective
> 1 September 2026. Prerequisites: [Facing Dancers](../02-language/formations.md#facing-dancers),
> [Ocean Wave](../02-language/formations.md#waves), [Hands Up hold](../02-language/hands-and-holds.md).

## Touch a Quarter

### At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Starting formation** | [Facing Dancers](../02-language/formations.md#facing-dancers) — **only** |
| **Ending formation** | Right-Hand [Mini-Wave](../02-language/formations.md#waves) |
| **Timing** | 2 beats |
| **Unit** | M42 of 50 |

### Animation

▶ **Play this call** — pause, step and scrub it: [Watching the animations](../08-reference/watching-the-animations.md).

### Dance action

> "In one smooth motion, Step to a Wave and Turn 1/4 by the Right"

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

### What you do

1. **Take the hand.** Step forward and join **your right hand with their right hand**, in a
   [Hands Up hold](../02-language/hands-and-holds.md) — forearms angled up, elbows near your sides.
2. **Step in.** As you take the hand, you are already closing the distance, so your feet move you into
   the middle of the pair.
3. **Turn a quarter turn by the right.** Keep the joined right hands where they are and walk a quarter
   of a circle around them, both of you turning in the same direction.
4. **Stop facing the other way** and lower your hands to the standard wave position.

You finish **side by side, facing opposite directions, right hands still joined** — a Right-Hand
Mini-Wave. The whole thing is one movement: the step and the turn are the same beat of music, not two.

### Timing

**2 beats** — one bar of music. It is quick, and it is meant to be: Touch a Quarter is a quarter of a
turn, the same distance your feet cover in a single walking step and a turn.

### Styling

> "Hands Up (see 'Styling: Hands and Arms', page 68). When called from Facing Couples, the four
> dancers do not make a Wave midway through Touch a Quarter."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

Keep the handhold light. Your right hands are the pivot, not a lever — you are not pulling your
partner round, you are both walking round the same joined hand.

### Restrictions

The manual gives the starting formation as **Facing Dancers only**, and adds that **the Ocean Wave
Rule does not apply to this call**. That means the caller may not use it in a phrase such as "Swing
Thru, All 8 Circulate, Touch a Quarter"; that combination is improper.

The left-handed version is a separate call, **Left Touch a Quarter**, and it steps to a Left-Hand
Wave and turns by the left.

### Common faults

| What happens | Why | The fix |
|---|---|---|
| You make a wave with all four dancers halfway through | Thinking of it as two calls | It is one movement; each pair just steps to its own Mini-Wave |
| You end up in a full Ocean Wave of four | Over-turning | A quarter turn is 90 degrees — stop there |
| The turn goes the wrong way | Turning left out of habit | Right hands joined means a **right**-hand turn |
| The handhold is high and stiff | Gripping instead of resting | Hands Up: forearms angled up, light grip, elbows in |

### Where you meet it

Touch a Quarter arrives with the wave-family units in the later part of the teaching order, and
quickly becomes the shortest way to get from a facing pair into a wave. You will hear it inside longer
phrases — "Heads Square Thru 4, Touch a Quarter, Scoot Back" is a very common one. See the
[course overview](../05-course/course-overview.md).

### Related calls

- **[Step to a Wave](m31-ocean-wave-family.md)** — the same step and turn, but starting from a
  distance.
- **[Single Hinge](m43-hinge-family.md)** — a half turn from a Mini-Wave instead of a quarter turn
  from facing dancers.
- **[Swing Thru](m33-swing-thru.md)** — a half turn by the right and then by the left.
- **[Pass the Ocean](m35-pass-the-ocean.md)** — a Touch a Quarter with the outside dancers pulled
  in.
- **[Box the Gnat](m30-box-the-gnat.md)** — a quarter-turn handhold that ends facing the same way
  rather than opposite.

### Also called / formerly

You will often hear the short form **"Touch 1/4"** or **"Touch a Quarter"** with no difference in
meaning. The left-handed version is always named in full as **Left Touch a Quarter**.
