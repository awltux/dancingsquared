# M44 — Centers In

> **Teaching unit M44** of the CALLERLAB (New) Mainstream Suggested Teaching Order, effective
> 1 September 2026. Prerequisites: [Centers and Ends](../02-language/naming-the-dancers.md),
> [Completed Double Pass Thru](m24-double-pass-thru.md),
> [Eight Chain Thru](../02-language/formations.md#eight-chain-thru).

## Centers In

### At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Starting formation** | [Completed Double Pass Thru](m24-double-pass-thru.md) or [Eight Chain Thru](../02-language/formations.md#eight-chain-thru); more generally, a Couple with their backs to the centre, each centre dancer looking straight at an outside dancer |
| **Ending formation** | Lines Facing Out from a Completed Double Pass Thru; Inverted Lines with Ends Facing from an Eight Chain Thru |
| **Timing** | 2 beats |
| **Unit** | M44 of 50 |

### Animation

▶ **Play this call** — pause, step and scrub it: [Watching the animations](../08-reference/watching-the-animations.md).

### Dance action

> "Without changing facing direction, the outside dancers step away from each other to make room for
> the center dancers, who step forward to end between them."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

### What you do

1. **Work out which you are.** The **centre dancers** are the couple whose backs are to the middle of
   the set. The **outside dancers** are the ones each centre dancer is looking at.
2. **Nobody turns.** This is the thing to hold on to: your facing direction never changes.
3. **If you are an outside dancer**, step sideways, away from your partner, to open a gap between you.
4. **If you are a centre dancer**, walk straight forward and stop in that gap.

The outsiders slide apart and the centres step between them. You finish as two lines of four, with the
former centres on the inside facing out.

### Timing

**2 beats** — one bar of music. It is short because only half the set moves, and they move in a
straight line.

### Styling

> "As center dancers step between the outside dancers they take hands with them, as appropriate
> (Couple handhold if facing the same direction or Ocean Wave styling if facing opposite directions)."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

Take the hand as you arrive, not before. If you are facing the same way as the dancer beside you, that
is an ordinary [couple handhold](../02-language/hands-and-holds.md); if you are facing the opposite
way, it is the wave-style [Hands Up hold](../02-language/hands-and-holds.md).

### Common faults

| What happens | Why | The fix |
|---|---|---|
| The centre dancers turn as they step in | Expecting to end facing the other way | Do **not** turn. Only your feet move |
| The outsiders stay put and get bumped | Forgetting they have a job | Outsiders step apart first — they make the room |
| You end up in a line facing in | Stepping too far and turning | Straight forward, one step, facing out as you already were |
| The two lines are too wide and the next call will not fit | The outsiders stepped too far | The outsiders step apart just enough for one dancer |

### Where you meet it

Centers In nearly always appears in the phrase **"Centers In, Cast Off Three-Quarters"**, which turns
a Completed Double Pass Thru into facing couples. It is taught with the
[Cast Off Three-Quarters](m45-cast-off-three-quarters.md) unit for that reason. See the
[course overview](../05-course/course-overview.md).

### Related calls

- **[Cast Off Three-Quarters](m45-cast-off-three-quarters.md)** — the call that almost always
  follows.
- **[Double Pass Thru](m24-double-pass-thru.md)** — the call that leaves you in the right formation
  to do this.
- **[Centers Trade](m32-trade-family.md)** — another call where only the centre pair moves.
- **[Split Two](m22-split-two.md)** — the outsides moving apart so the centres can pass between
  them.

### Also called / formerly

You may hear **"Put Centers In"**, which is exactly the same call.
