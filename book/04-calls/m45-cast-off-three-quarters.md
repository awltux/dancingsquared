# M45 — Cast Off Three-Quarters

> **Teaching unit M45** of the CALLERLAB (New) Mainstream Suggested Teaching Order, effective
> 1 September 2026. Prerequisites: [Mini-Wave](../02-language/formations.md#waves),
> [Centers and Ends](../02-language/naming-the-dancers.md), [Centers In](m44-centers-in.md).

## Cast Off Three-Quarters

### At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Starting formation** | [Mini-Wave](../02-language/formations.md#waves), or a General Line made of a Mini-Wave and a Couple |
| **Ending formation** | A Mini-Wave from a Mini-Wave; Facing Couples from a One-Faced or Two-Faced Line; a General Box from a General Line |
| **Timing** | 6 beats |
| **Unit** | M45 of 50 |

### Animation

▶ **Play this call** — pause, step and scrub it: [Watching the animations](../08-reference/watching-the-animations.md).

### Dance action

> "From a Mini-Wave, Turn 3/4.
> From a General Line, each half of the line works together: those in a Mini-Wave Turn 3/4 while
> those in a Couple turn, as a unit, away from the center of the Line for 3/4 of a circle (270
> degrees)."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

### What you do

1. **Find which half of the line you are in.**
2. **If you are in the Mini-Wave**, keep your joined hands and turn **three-quarters of a turn** — 270
   degrees — around the centre of your Mini-Wave. You stop before you have completed the circle, so
   you are now facing a new direction.
3. **If you are in the Couple**, stay with your partner, keep your couple handhold, and walk as a unit
   three-quarters of a circle, **away from the centre of the line**.
4. **Adjust to your new formation.** From a Two-Faced or One-Faced Line you finish as Facing Couples.

Three-quarters is the awkward part. You turn past a half turn but stop short of a full one, so you do
**not** end up where you started and you do **not** end up facing the opposite way.

### Timing

**6 beats** — three bars of music, the same length as a [Swing Thru](m33-swing-thru.md). Take it
slowly and let the beat carry the turn; rushing a three-quarter turn is how dancers end up in the
wrong place.

### Styling

> "Dancers who are partners maintain current handholds throughout the call."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

Do not let go halfway round, and do not grab a new hand to help yourself round. The handhold you
started with is the one you keep.

### Restrictions

The manual lists the starting formations as **a Mini-Wave or a General Line**, and records one
improper combination: **it is improper to Sweep a Quarter after Cast Off Three-Quarters**. The caller
should not put those two together.

There is also a long-standing disagreement about where the pivot point sits when a couple turns.
The 2026 manual settles it: **as long as you turn in the right direction and finish in the right
formation, you are dancing the call properly.**

### Common faults

| What happens | Why | The fix |
|---|---|---|
| You turn a full circle and are back where you started | Three-quarters slides into a full turn | Stop a quarter short — 270 degrees, facing a new wall |
| You turn a half turn | Confusing it with a half Trade | It is **three**-quarters, not a half |
| The couple turns toward the centre of the line | Turning the wrong way | The Couple turns **away** from the centre of the line |
| You end up facing the wrong wall in a Two-Faced Line | The Couple and the Mini-Wave turned different amounts | Every dancer turns three-quarters; the two halves just dance it differently |

### Where you meet it

Cast Off Three-Quarters is most often heard in the pair **"Centers In, Cast Off Three-Quarters"**,
which takes a Completed Double Pass Thru to Facing Couples. It also appears as simply **"Cast Off
3/4"**. See the [course overview](../05-course/course-overview.md).

### Related calls

- **[Centers In](m44-centers-in.md)** — the call that normally sets it up.
- **[Single Hinge](m43-hinge-family.md)** — one half turn from the same Mini-Wave, where this is
  three-quarters.
- **[Swing Thru](m33-swing-thru.md)** — the same six beats of turning, by the right and then the
  left.
- **[Wheel and Deal](m26-wheel-and-deal.md)** — two couples turning as units, like the Couple half
  of this call.

### Also called / formerly

You will nearly always hear the short form **"Cast Off 3/4"**. There are other fractions in the wider
dance world — Half Cast Off, Cast Off a Full — but at Mainstream this unit is the three-quarter turn
and nothing else.
