# M46 — Spin the Top

> **Teaching unit M46** of the CALLERLAB (New) Mainstream Suggested Teaching Order, effective
> 1 September 2026. Prerequisites: [Ocean Wave](../02-language/formations.md#waves),
> [Centers and Ends](../02-language/naming-the-dancers.md), [Swing Thru](m33-swing-thru.md).

## Spin the Top

### At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Starting formation** | [Ocean Wave](../02-language/formations.md#waves) |
| **Ending formation** | Ocean Wave **perpendicular** to the starting formation |
| **Timing** | 8 beats |
| **Unit** | M46 of 50 |

### Animation

▶ **Play this call** — pause, step and scrub it: [Watching the animations](../08-reference/watching-the-animations.md).

### Dance action

> "Each end and adjacent center Turn 1/2. New centers Turn 3/4 while the ends move forward in a
> quarter circle around the formation to become ends of the final Ocean Wave."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

### What you do

1. **Start in a wave.** You are one of four dancers in an [Ocean Wave](../02-language/formations.md#waves) —
   two centres in the middle, one end on each side.
2. **Ends and the adjacent centre turn a half turn.** Each end takes the hand of the centre beside it
   and turns half way round, by the same hand you are already holding.
3. **Now you have new centres.** After that half turn, the two dancers standing in the middle are the
   ones who must turn again.
4. **The new centres turn three-quarters** — 270 degrees — around each other.
5. **The ends, meanwhile, walk forward** in a quarter circle around the outside of the formation, and
   stop as the ends of the new wave.

You finish in a wave that points **across** the one you started in — at right angles to it. If you
started in a wave running left to right, you finish in a wave running toward and away from you.

### Timing

**8 beats** — four bars of music. It is the longest call in this group, because it is really two
turns and a walk packed into one command. The animation runs over seven and a half beats,
which is why the track stops just short of the fourth bar.

### Styling

> "Use same styling as in Swing Thru."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

In practice that means the same light forearm [handhold](../02-language/hands-and-holds.md) you use
for a Swing Thru, released as dancers move on to their next partner. Keep your arms relaxed and let
the turn carry you.

### Restrictions

Spin the Top starts from an **Ocean Wave**. From a **Right-Hand Ocean Wave** the phrases **"Left Spin
the Top"** and **"Centers Start, Spin the Top"** are **improper**, because neither tells the dancers
anything true about the call.

From a Left-Hand Ocean Wave you may hear **"Left Spin the Top"**; that is acceptable but not
required. More often, "Left Spin the Top" is used from facing couples, to mean "step to a Left-Hand
Wave first".

### Common faults

| What happens | Why | The fix |
|---|---|---|
| You finish in the same wave you started in | Turning the second part a half instead of three-quarters | The new centres turn **three-quarters**, not a half |
| The ends stay put | Forgetting that the ends keep moving | The ends walk a quarter circle around the outside while the centres turn |
| Both parts turn the same amount | Missing that it is two different turns | Ends and adjacent centres: **1/2**. New centres: **3/4** |
| You try to turn with the wrong partner | Not noticing who the new centres are | After the first half turn, look again — the middle pair has changed |

### Where you meet it

Spin the Top is a wave-to-wave call, so you will meet it wherever a caller wants to swing the wave
round through a right angle — often after a [Pass the Ocean](m35-pass-the-ocean.md) or an
[Extend](m36-extend.md). Callers sometimes say **"Spin the Top; Spin it again"**, which means dance it
twice. See the [course overview](../05-course/course-overview.md).

### Related calls

- **[Swing Thru](m33-swing-thru.md)** — turn half by the right, then half by the left.
- **[Cast Off Three-Quarters](m45-cast-off-three-quarters.md)** — the same three-quarter turn done
  from a stationary line.
- **[Pass the Ocean](m35-pass-the-ocean.md)** — the usual way to build the wave you start from.
- **[Scoot Back](m47-scoot-back.md)** — another wave-to-wave call with an arm turn in the middle.

### Also called / formerly

Some callers say **"Spin your Top"**. It is the same call.
