# M47 — Scoot Back

> **Teaching unit M47** of the CALLERLAB (New) Mainstream Suggested Teaching Order, effective
> 1 September 2026. Prerequisites: [Box Circulate](m19-circulate-family.md),
> [Ocean Wave](../02-language/formations.md#waves), [Extend](m36-extend.md),
> [Arm Turn](../02-language/hands-and-holds.md).

## Scoot Back

### At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Starting formation** | Box Circulate, 1/4 Tag, or Single 1/4 Tag |
| **Ending formation** | Box Circulate, 3/4 Tag, or Single 3/4 Tag |
| **Timing** | 6 beats |
| **Unit** | M47 of 50 |

### Animation

▶ **Play this call** — pause, step and scrub it: [Watching the animations](../08-reference/watching-the-animations.md).

### Dance action

> "From a Box Circulate: Trailers Extend, Arm Turn 1/2, Extend while Leaders Box Circulate
> From a 1/4 Tag or Single 1/4 Tag: Extend, Arm Turn 1/2, Extend"

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

### What you do

From a box, the two halves of the call happen at the same time — so you only do the half that belongs
to you.

1. **Find out whether you are a Leader or a Trailer.** In a box, the Leaders face out and the Trailers
   face in. See [Leaders and Trailers](../02-language/naming-the-dancers.md).
2. **If you are a Trailer:** reach forward, take the hand of the dancer in front of you, turn half a
   turn with them, then move forward again. That is *Extend, Arm Turn 1/2, Extend*.
3. **If you are a Leader:** simply [Box Circulate](m19-circulate-family.md) — walk forward to the next
   spot in the box.
4. **Keep the same hand free.** You take a hand at the start, use it for the arm turn, and take it
   again at the end.

The two Trailers meet in the middle and turn; the two Leaders walk round the outside. From a 1/4 Tag
the same idea applies, but every dancer does the Extend – Arm Turn – Extend pattern, because a 1/4 Tag
is a box with two extra dancers attached.

### Timing

**6 beats** — three bars of music. The arm turn in the middle takes the middle two beats; the two
Extends take one beat each either side, with the rest going on the step forward.

### Styling

> "Those who Arm Turn 1/2 use a forearm handhold. Dancers must keep the same hand available, because
> it is used in the starting formation, the Arm Turn, and the ending formation (for Trailers in a Box
> Circulate and Centers of a Single 1/4 Tag)."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

Take a **forearm** grip for the turn, not a handshake. And do not close the hand you need: the same
hand does all three jobs in this call.

From a left-handed formation the arm turn is left-handed and the ending formation is left-handed.
Callers sometimes help with **"Scoot Back, use the left"**.

### Restrictions

Scoot Back is a **four-dancer call, and both dancers in each pair take part**. That makes some naming
improper: after "Heads Square Thru, Touch a Quarter", it would be **improper to call "Boys Scoot
Back"**, because the action genuinely needs the Girls as well. It is proper only where the named
dancers really are the ones who turn — for example **"Boys Scoot Back" from Ocean Waves with the Boys
in the centre**.

Scoot Back is also a call of four dancers. From Columns, dancers form two Box Circulates and each box
does the call separately.

### Common faults

| What happens | Why | The fix |
|---|---|---|
| The Leaders also try to turn | Thinking everyone does everything | Leaders Box Circulate; only the Trailers turn |
| You use a handshake instead of a forearm | Habit from other calls | The turn is a forearm hold |
| You close the hand you need for the Extend | Not noticing it is the same hand throughout | Keep that hand ready from the start |
| You end up facing the wrong way | Turning a full turn instead of a half | Arm Turn **1/2** — you finish facing the opposite direction |

### Where you meet it

Scoot Back is a standard way to rotate a wave or a box, and it appears often right after
[Touch a Quarter](m42-touch-a-quarter.md) — "Heads Square Thru, Touch a Quarter, Scoot Back" is a
phrase worth learning as a shape. See the [course overview](../05-course/course-overview.md).

### Related calls

- **[Box Circulate](m19-circulate-family.md)** — exactly what the Leaders do while the Trailers
  turn.
- **[Extend](m36-extend.md)** — the step forward before and after the turn.
- **[Touch a Quarter](m42-touch-a-quarter.md)** — a common call just before it.
- **[Spin the Top](m46-spin-the-top.md)** — the neighbouring wave call with a three-quarter centre

### Also called / formerly

Scoot Back has always been called **Scoot Back**. Callers add helping words for clarity — "Scoot Back,
use the left", or "Scoot Back, Boys go in" — so listen for the extra phrase.
