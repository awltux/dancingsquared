# M48 — Fold / Cross Fold

> **Teaching unit M48** of the CALLERLAB (New) Mainstream Suggested Teaching Order, effective
> 1 September 2026. Prerequisites: [Couple](../02-language/formations.md#couple),
> [Mini-Wave](../02-language/formations.md#waves),
> [Centers and Ends](../02-language/naming-the-dancers.md).

The manual gives Fold and Cross Fold as two parts of one family, and this teaching unit treats them
together. They differ only in how far the folding dancer walks.

## Fold / Cross Fold

### At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Starting formation** | A [Couple](../02-language/formations.md#couple) or a Mini-Wave (Fold); a General Line (Cross Fold) |
| **Ending formation** | Facing Dancers or a Tandem (Fold); see the comments for Cross Fold |
| **Timing** | 2 beats (Fold); 4 beats (Cross Fold) |
| **Unit** | M48 of 50 |

### Animation

▶ **Play this call** — pause, step and scrub it: [Watching the animations](../08-reference/watching-the-animations.md).

### Dance action

Fold:

> "The designated dancer steps forward and walks in a semi-circle toward the adjacent dancer to end
> facing that position. The non-designated dancer is inactive and does not move."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

Cross Fold:

> "Designated dancers step forward and walk in a semicircle, passing both the center of the Line and
> one dancer position, to end facing the position of the next dancer. Non-designated dancers are
> inactive and do not move."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

### What you do

The caller always names who folds — "Boys Fold", "Ends Fold", "Girls Cross Fold". If you are not
named, you do nothing at all.

**Fold**

1. **If you are named**, step forward, then walk a **semicircle** — half a circle — toward the dancer
   next to you.
2. **Stop facing their position.** You end up facing the spot where that dancer is standing.
3. **If you are not named**, stand still. Do not help, do not turn round, do not move.

**Cross Fold**

1. **If you are named**, step forward and walk a **bigger semicircle**: past the centre of the line
   **and** past one dancer position.
2. **Stop facing the next dancer along.**

The difference in one line: Fold takes you to your neighbour's spot; Cross Fold carries you one
position further, so you end up facing somebody two places away.

### Timing

**2 beats** for Fold — one bar. **4 beats** for Cross Fold — two bars, because the walk is about twice
as long. Moving on the music matters here: a Fold that takes four beats leaves your partner standing
awkwardly while everyone waits.

### Styling

> "None"

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

That is the manual's whole entry for both parts, and it means what it says: there is no handhold to
keep and no grip to form. Walk it smoothly, and arrive facing the right spot rather than drifting to
a halt facing the wrong one.

### Common faults

| What happens | Why | The fix |
|---|---|---|
| The inactive dancer moves too | Being helpful | If you are not named, you are furniture. Stand still |
| You end up beside your neighbour, facing the same way | Cutting the semicircle short | Walk the whole half circle and finish **facing** their position |
| Fold and Cross Fold get mixed up | Only one word separates them | Fold = one position. Cross Fold = past the centre and one position |
| You turn on the spot instead of walking | Thinking of it as a face | It is a walk, not a turn — your feet travel a semicircle |

### Where you meet it

Fold appears again immediately inside [Recycle](m50-recycle.md), where the ends Cross Fold and the
centres Fold. That is why it is worth learning carefully now. See the
[course overview](../05-course/course-overview.md).

### Related calls

- **[Recycle](m50-recycle.md)** — built directly on Fold and Cross Fold.
- **[Run / Cross Run](m34-run-cross-run.md)** — the same semicircle idea, danced into a new position
  rather than facing one.
- **[Zoom](m41-zoom.md)** — the other half-call built on a circle, but with a full turn.
- **[Cast Off Three-Quarters](m45-cast-off-three-quarters.md)** — a turn from a line, where Fold is
  a walk.

### Also called / formerly

The manual records that **"Everyone Fold"** or **"Everybody Fold to Face"** is a **gimmick** — with
everybody folding there is no inactive dancer to fold toward, so callers who use it expect it to end
in Facing Dancers. It may not be used from a Couple. If you hear it, treat it as described and expect
the caller to explain.
