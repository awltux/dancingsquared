# M49 — Tag the Line Family

> **Teaching unit M49** of the CALLERLAB (New) Mainstream Suggested Teaching Order, effective
> 1 September 2026. Prerequisites: [General Line](../02-language/formations.md#lines),
> [Centers and Ends](../02-language/naming-the-dancers.md),
> [Passing Rule](../03-rules/passing-rule.md).

This unit is a **family** with two lettered parts, and each part gets its own call card below. Both
start the same way: everyone turns to face the middle of the line and walks forward, passing right
shoulders. The difference is **when you stop**.

## a. Tag the Line (In / Out / Left / Right)

### At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Starting formation** | General Line |
| **Ending formation** | Back-to-back Tandems; with a direction given, it depends on the direction — "Tag the Line, Face Right" ends in a Right-Hand Two-Faced Line |
| **Timing** | 6 beats |
| **Unit** | M49 of 50, part a |

### Animation

▶ **Play this call** — pause, step and scrub it: [Watching the animations](../08-reference/watching-the-animations.md).

### Dance action

> "Each dancer turns 90 degrees, in place, to face the center of the Line (some dancers think of this
> as turning toward the far end of the Line) and then walks forward, passing right shoulders with all
> dancers in the other half of the Line, until the last dancers in each half have passed each other.
> If a facing direction is given, each dancer then turns 90 degrees, in place, toward the specified
> direction."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

### What you do

1. **Turn in place to face the centre of your line.** Turn the short way — toward the middle, not the
   long way round.
2. **Walk forward.** Everyone walks at the same time, straight past each other.
3. **Pass right shoulders** with every dancer coming the other way, obeying the
   [Passing Rule](../03-rules/passing-rule.md).
4. **Keep walking until the last two have passed.** Not until you have passed one person — until the
   end dancers have gone by each other.
5. **Stop.** You are now standing back to back in two Tandems — two dancers facing one way, two facing
   the other, in a single line.

If the caller adds a direction — **"Tag the Line, Face Right"** or **"Tag the Line, Face In"** — then
after you stop you turn 90 degrees, on the spot, to face that way. **"In"** means toward the centre of
the set; **"Out"** means away from it.

### Timing

**6 beats** — three bars of music. The animation runs over five and a half beats, so the
track finishes a little inside the third bar.

### Styling

> "Arms in natural dance position, hands ready to assume appropriate position for the next call. If the
> Centers start holding right hands, a flowing type of movement can be achieved by taking a slight
> step forward while turning toward the center of the Line."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

Keep your arms down as you walk. The hands come up again only when the next call starts.

### Restrictions

This is a **four-dancer call**. From a General Line of eight, the phrase **"Each Side"** or **"Each
Four"** is helpful but not required.

The manual records one clear restriction: **the application from a line of two dancers is improper at
Mainstream** — that version has its own name and lives in another program.

The direction given does not have to be the same for everyone. **"Tag the Line, Boys Face Left, Girls
Face Right"** is legal.

### Common faults

| What happens | Why | The fix |
|---|---|---|
| Everyone stops after passing one person | Thinking of it as a Pass Thru | Walk until the **last** dancers in each half have passed |
| You pass left shoulders | Forgetting the passing rule | Right shoulders, every dancer, every time |
| You turn the long way to face the centre | Turning outward | Turn in place, the short way, toward the middle |
| After "Face Right" you are in the wrong spot | Turning and walking at the same time | Stop first, then turn 90 degrees on the spot |

### Where you meet it

Tag the Line is one of the standard ways to convert a line into columns or into facing lines, and it
appears constantly near the end of the teaching order. See the
[course overview](../05-course/course-overview.md).

### Related calls

- [Fraction Tag](#b-fraction-14-12-34-tag) — the same walk, stopped early, below.
- **[Double Pass Thru](m24-double-pass-thru.md)** — the same right-shoulder passing, but from
  columns rather than a line.
- **[Trade By](m39-trade-by.md)** — another line-to-line call that uses right shoulders.
- **[Wheel and Deal](m26-wheel-and-deal.md)** — often used to turn what Tag the Line leaves behind.

### Also called / formerly

You may hear **"Tag the Line All the Way"**, which is simply Tag the Line with no direction. The
directions **In**, **Out**, **Left** and **Right** are the four common endings.

## b. Fraction (1/4, 1/2, 3/4) Tag

### At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Starting formation** | General Line |
| **Ending formation** | 1/4 Tag ends in a Right-Hand Single 1/4 Tag; 1/2 Tag ends in a Right-Hand Box Circulate; 3/4 Tag ends in a Right-Hand Single 3/4 Tag |
| **Timing** | 1/4 Tag: 3 beats; 1/2 Tag: 4 beats; 3/4 Tag: 5 beats |
| **Unit** | M49 of 50, part b |

### Animation

▶ **Play this call** — pause, step and scrub it: [Watching the animations](../08-reference/watching-the-animations.md).

### Dance action

> "Each dancer turns 90 degrees, in place, to face the center of the Line (some dancers think of this
> as turning toward the far end of the Line) and starts walking forward, as necessary passing right
> shoulders with dancers from the other half of the line. For 1/4, 1/2, and 3/4, dancers stop at a
> Right-Hand Single 1/4 Tag, Right-Hand Box Circulate, and Right-Hand Single 3/4 Tag formation,
> respectively."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

### What you do

The start is the same as Tag the Line: turn in place to face the middle, then walk forward, passing
right shoulders.

1. **Turn to face the centre of the line.**
2. **Walk forward**, passing right shoulders.
3. **Stop when the caller's fraction is done** — a quarter, a half, or three-quarters of the way.

The fraction tells you **how much of the line you have walked through**:

- **1/4 Tag** — you walk a quarter of the way and stop in a Right-Hand Single 1/4 Tag.
- **1/2 Tag** — you walk half the way and stop in a Right-Hand Box Circulate.
- **3/4 Tag** — you walk three-quarters of the way and stop in a Right-Hand Single 3/4 Tag.

Because you stop part way, the four of you are no longer in a straight line — you are in a wave or a
box, still holding right hands.

### Timing

**3 beats** for a 1/4 Tag, **4 beats** for a 1/2 Tag, **5 beats** for a 3/4 Tag. The animation
draws Half Tag over three and a half beats, just inside the four the manual asks for.

### Styling

> "If the centers start holding right hands, a flowing type of movement can be achieved by taking a
> slight step forward while turning toward the center of the Line."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

Keep the right-hand grip you formed as you passed. A Fraction Tag finishes *with* hands joined, unlike
the full Tag the Line, which finishes hands-free back to back.

### Common faults

| What happens | Why | The fix |
|---|---|---|
| You dance a full Tag the Line | Habit from the previous unit | Listen for the fraction — it tells you where to stop |
| You stop, then keep drifting forward | Momentum | Stop where the fraction puts you, and set your feet |
| The four of you end up in a straight line | Walking to the end anyway | A Fraction Tag finishes in a wave or a box, not a line |
| You let go of the right hand | Thinking all Tags finish hands-free | Keep the right-hand hold; that is the formation you finish in |

### Where you meet it

The fractions are most often heard as **"Half Tag"** and **"Quarter Tag"**, and they are the standard
way to get from a line into a wave or a box ready for the next call. See the
[course overview](../05-course/course-overview.md).

### Related calls

- [Tag the Line](#a-tag-the-line-in-out-left-right) — the full version, above.
- **Extend** — the step forward out of a 1/4 Tag. See [M36](m36-extend.md).
- **Pass the Ocean** — another way into the same wave formations. See [M35](m35-pass-the-ocean.md).
- **Scoot Back** — a common next call from a 1/4 Tag. See [M47](m47-scoot-back.md).

### Also called / formerly

Callers nearly always abbreviate: **"Half Tag"** for Half Tag the Line, **"Quarter Tag"** for a
quarter. The manual records that **"Tag the Line One-Quarter" is strongly discouraged**, because the
call takes only three beats — the fraction alone says it faster.
