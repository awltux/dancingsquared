# M50 — Recycle (from Waves only)

> **Teaching unit M50** of the CALLERLAB (New) Mainstream Suggested Teaching Order, effective
> 1 September 2026. Prerequisites: [Ocean Wave](../02-language/formations.md#waves),
> [Centers and Ends](../02-language/naming-the-dancers.md),
> [Fold / Cross Fold](m48-fold-cross-fold.md).

The unit title carries the restriction with it: at Mainstream, Recycle is **from waves only**.

## Recycle

### At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Starting formation** | [Ocean Wave](../02-language/formations.md#waves) — **only** |
| **Ending formation** | [Facing Couples](../02-language/formations.md#facing-couples) |
| **Timing** | 4 beats |
| **Unit** | M50 of 50 |

### Animation

▶ **Play this call** — pause, step and scrub it: [Watching the animations](../08-reference/watching-the-animations.md).

### Dance action

> "Ends Cross Fold. Meanwhile, each Center Folds behind the adjacent End and follows the End around,
> adjusting to finish side-by-side in Facing Couples."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

### What you do

The two dancers at the ends and the two in the middle do different things, at the same time.

1. **Look at the wave.** Are you an **End** (on the outside) or a **Centre** (in the middle)?
2. **If you are an End**, Cross Fold: step forward and walk a big semicircle, past the centre of the
   formation and past one position, until you are facing the spot you are heading for.
3. **If you are a Centre**, Fold behind the End next to you, then **follow that End round** — walk
   almost a full circle behind them, staying on the inside of their turn.
4. **Come out side by side.** As the turn finishes, the End and the Centre settle next to each other,
   both facing the same way.

You finish as **Facing Couples** — two pairs, each facing the other pair. The manual sums up the
Centre's job in three words worth memorising: **Fold, follow, face.**

A Centre walks almost a full circle and finishes a small step back from where they started, beside
the dancer they followed.

### Timing

**4 beats** — two bars of music. Everybody is walking a circle of some size, so it flows if you move
on the beat rather than stopping to think halfway round.

### Styling

> "As each End and adjacent Center begin to turn, they release their Mini-Wave handhold. They
> smoothly establish a Couple handhold once they are approximately facing the same direction."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

Let go of the wave grip **as you start to turn** — holding on through the circle will drag you both
off course. Pick up the ordinary [couple handhold](../02-language/hands-and-holds.md) when you are
nearly facing the same way, not before.

### Restrictions

Recycle is defined **from waves only**. The manual gives the starting formation as **Ocean Wave** and
is explicit about what that rules out:

> "The Facing Couples Rule does not apply. From Facing Couples, the call Recycle has a different
> definition that is not part of the Mainstream program."

— CALLERLAB, *Mainstream Dance Program Definitions*, revised 29 March 2026.

So if you are standing in facing couples, do not attempt the wave version. At Mainstream the caller
will set up the wave first.

### Common faults

| What happens | Why | The fix |
|---|---|---|
| You try it from facing couples | Assuming the call works anywhere | It is waves only. Wait for the wave |
| The Centres turn the wrong way | Forgetting which End to follow | Each Centre folds behind the **adjacent** End and follows that End |
| The Centres stop halfway round | Dancing a Fold instead of the follow | Keep walking — almost a full circle — then face |
| The pair drifts apart | Holding the wave grip too long | Release the Mini-Wave handhold as the turn begins |
| You finish in a line instead of facing couples | Not adjusting at the end | Each End and Centre settles side by side, pairs facing pairs |

### Where you meet it

Recycle is the last unit in the teaching order, and it is a workhorse: it is one of the standard ways
to get out of a wave and back into facing couples so the next call can start cleanly. You will meet it
constantly once you can dance waves. See the [course overview](../05-course/course-overview.md).

### Also called / formerly

Recycle has always been called **Recycle**. The **left-handed** version is called from a Left-Hand
Ocean Wave and is danced the same way, mirrored. There is a separate Facing Couples Recycle in other
programs — as the manual says, it is **not part of Mainstream**, so if you hear it, it is not this
call.
