# Class 1 — The First Evening

> **Units covered:** M1, M2, M3, M4. **Units assumed:** none. Every dancer arrives knowing nothing, so
> assume nothing and take nothing for granted.

## The units in this class

| Unit | Call | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| [M1](../04-calls/m01-circle-family.md) | Circle Left/Right (1/4, 1/2, 3/4, Full) | moves the whole square round a ring together |
| [M2](../04-calls/m02-dosado.md) | Dosado | exchanges two facing dancers and brings them back facing in |
| [M3](../04-calls/m03-promenade-family.md) | Couples Promenade | moves every couple round the square, side by side |
| [M4](../04-calls/m04-arm-turn-family.md) | Allemande Left | turns you by the left forearm to face your corner |

## Before you start

- The class can stand in a squared set, and knows which wall is which.
- They know the words **partner**, **corner**, **Head** and **Side**.
- They can hold hands in a ring without gripping, and walk in time to the music.

## Teaching sequence

1. **M1 — Circle Left/Right (1/4, 1/2, 3/4, Full).** Teach the step first, the handhold second: walk a
   ring with no hands, then join hands and repeat. Pre-empt gripped, stretched rings.
2. **M2 — Dosado.** Demonstrate with one couple, then send everybody pass-to-pass and stop halfway so
   they see themselves back to back. Pre-empt the drift outwards.
3. **M3 — Couples Promenade.** Demonstrate the promenade handhold and promenade direction before
   anything else. Pre-empt the couple who walk opposite ways.
4. **M4 — Allemande Left.** Have one pair show the elbow grip, then run the whole square. Pre-empt the
   tight, wrestling allemande: the grip rests, it does not pull.

## Drills

1. Circle Left 1/4
   Dosado
   Allemande Left
   Promenade Home

   Trains the whole evening's work in one figure.

2. Circle Left (Full)
   Forward and Back
   Dosado
   Allemande Left
   Promenade Home

   Trains the same calls after a movement that changes nothing but timing.

## Checks

- Hands come apart, or the ring turns oval — say "light hands".
- Dancers look at the floor through Dosado — walk it half speed, heads up.
- Promenaders drift into the middle — fix the direction with your arm.
- Allemande Left ends in a shove — the grip is a hook, not a hold.
