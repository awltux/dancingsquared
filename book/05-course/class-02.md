# Class 2 — Stars, Grands and Wheels

> **Units covered:** M5, M6, M7, M8. **Units assumed:** M1–M4 — the ring, Dosado, the promenade and
> the allemande.

## The units in this class

| Unit | Call | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| [M5](../04-calls/m05-star-right-left.md) | Star Right/Left | turns four dancers round a centre by one hand |
| [M6](../04-calls/m06-right-and-left-grand-family.md) | Right and Left Grand | weaves the whole square past each other by alternate hands |
| [M7](../04-calls/m07-pass-thru.md) | Pass Thru | walks two facing dancers past each other |
| [M8](../04-calls/m08-wheel-around.md) | Wheel Around | turns a couple about, side by side |

## Before you start

- They can square up, circle and promenade without being told the direction.
- They can find their corner without counting round the square.
- They hold hands lightly, and let go when a call says to.

## Teaching sequence

1. **M5 — Star Right/Left.** Show the star handhold at the centre, hands stacked, not a heap, and have
   them walk it without turning their shoulders. Pre-empt the dancer who drifts outwards.
2. **M6 — Right and Left Grand.** Walk the first half slowly and stop when everyone faces their
   partner. Pre-empt the two mistakes that always appear: following the person you just left, and
   reaching with the wrong hand.
3. **M7 — Pass Thru.** The simplest call of the evening, so use it as a rest: two steps, past, stop.
   Pre-empt the pair who stop level and edge sideways.
4. **M8 — Wheel Around.** Teach it from a promenade, so the couple have a direction to keep, and show
   the outside dancer backing round. Pre-empt the couple who turn two different ways.

## Drills

1. Circle Left 1/4
   Allemande Left
   Right and Left Grand
   Promenade Home

   Trains the allemande-to-grand join that closes almost every tip.

2. Heads Pass Thru
   Heads Wheel Around
   Heads Pass Thru
   Heads Wheel Around

   Trains Pass Thru and Wheel Around together, returning the Heads home.

3. Star Right (Full)
   Star Left (Full)
   Allemande Left
   Promenade Home

   Trains the star handhold and the change from right hand to left.

## Checks

- The star collapses — hands are pulled down; raise them to eye level.
- Dancers follow the last hand they touched in the Grand — stop and face the next hand.
- Pass Thru ends on each other's toes — two full steps, not one long lean.
- Wheel Around becomes a wrestle — say which dancer walks backwards.
