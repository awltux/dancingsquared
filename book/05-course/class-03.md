# Class 3 — The Couple Calls

> **Units covered:** M9, M10, M11, M12. **Units assumed:** M1–M8 — the ring, the allemande, the
> grand, Pass Thru and Wheel Around.

## The units in this class

| Unit | Call | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| [M9](../04-calls/m09-courtesy-turn.md) | Courtesy Turn | turns a facing pair into a couple, hands joined |
| [M10](../04-calls/m10-right-and-left-thru.md) | Right and Left Thru | passes a facing couple and turns them round together |
| [M11](../04-calls/m11-swing.md) | Swing | turns your partner in a closed hold |
| [M12](../04-calls/m12-ladies-chain-family.md) | Ladies Chain | sends the Girls across for a Courtesy Turn and back |

## Before you start

- They can Pass Thru and finish back to back without being told twice.
- They can turn a quarter in place and stay on their own spot.
- They know their partner from their corner without hesitating.

## Teaching sequence

1. **M9 — Courtesy Turn.** Teach this before any call that uses it: one couple in the middle, turning
   slowly, while the class watches the joined hands. Pre-empt the Boy who steers with his arm.
2. **M10 — Right and Left Thru.** Walk it once without saying the name: it is a Pass Thru plus a
   Courtesy Turn. Pre-empt the couple who drift diagonally across the square.
3. **M11 — Swing.** Show the hold before the turn, and swing with the class in two halves so you can
   watch. Pre-empt the dancer who leans back and lets their partner carry them.
4. **M12 — Ladies Chain.** Teach the hand the Girl offers as she crosses, and wait for the Boys to
   form the Courtesy Turn before the chain goes back. Pre-empt the Girl who starts back too early.

## Drills

1. Heads Right and Left Thru
   Heads Ladies Chain
   Heads Swing
   Promenade Home

   Trains the three couple calls in the order callers most often use them.

2. Heads Pass Thru
   Heads Right and Left Thru
   Heads Pass Thru
   Heads Right and Left Thru

   Trains Pass Thru into the Courtesy Turn until the turn is automatic.

3. Heads Pass Thru
   Heads Wheel Around
   Allemande Left
   Promenade Home

   Trains the closing figure, after a short piece of Heads dancing.

## Checks

- The Courtesy Turn finishes with hands apart — keep the hands joined all the way.
- Right and Left Thru ends facing the wrong wall — watch the feet.
- Swings look like a race — hum the beat and swing again slower.
- Ladies Chain returns the Girl facing out — walk it once with two couples only.
