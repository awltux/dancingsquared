# Class 4 — Passing and Leading

> **Units covered:** M13, M14, M15, M16. **Units assumed:** M1–M12 — including the Courtesy Turn,
> which every turn-back and lead-out call is built on.

## The units in this class

| Unit | Call | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| [M13](../04-calls/m13-half-sashay-family.md) | Half Sashay | swaps a couple's two dancers without letting go |
| [M14](../04-calls/m14-turn-back-family.md) | U-Turn Back | turns one dancer about on the spot |
| [M15](../04-calls/m15-separate-family.md) | Separate | sends a named couple out and round behind |
| [M16](../04-calls/m16-lead-right-lead-left.md) | Lead Right / Lead Left | takes a couple out and round a quarter turn |

## Before you start

- They can Courtesy Turn with the hands kept joined the whole way.
- They know which dancer of a couple stands on which side.
- They hear "Heads" and "Sides" and act on it without a second telling.

## Teaching sequence

1. **M13 — Half Sashay.** Show the couple handing through each other, hands still joined. Pre-empt the
   pair who let go, walk round and rejoin facing the wrong way.
2. **M14 — U-Turn Back.** Turn about hands off first, then with a partner's hand, so the class sees the
   handhold survive. Pre-empt the dancer who turns the short way and ends inside out.
3. **M15 — Separate.** Walk the active couple out, round and in while the others stand still. Say that
   the dancers not named do **nothing** — the first inactive half in the course.
4. **M16 — Lead Right.** Send a couple out and round. Pre-empt the couple who walk out and stop: the
   call is out **and** a quarter turn, and the turn is what finishes it.

## Drills

1. Heads Lead Right
   Heads Half Sashay
   Heads U-Turn Back
   Heads Lead Left
   Allemande Left
   Promenade Home

   Trains the turn-and-lead calls with the sashay between them.

2. Heads Separate Around 1 to a Line
   Heads Forward and Back
   Heads Pass Thru
   Heads U-Turn Back
   Heads Pass Thru
   Allemande Left
   Promenade Home

   Trains Separate into a line, then the U-Turn Back that reverses it.

3. Circle Left 1/4
   Dosado
   Allemande Left
   Right and Left Grand
   Promenade Home

   Trains the whole course so far as a closing figure.

## Checks

- Half Sashay ends facing out — they let go and stepped round; keep the hands joined.
- U-Turn Back pulls the partner off their spot — do it hands off.
- Separate Around 1 to a Line comes back the wrong length — count as they arrive.
- Lead Right finishes facing the middle — the quarter turn was missed; name the wall.
