# Class 5 — Veer, Star Thru and the Circulate

> **Units covered:** M17, M18, M19, M20. **Units assumed:** M1–M16 — in particular Lead Right, which
> is how most of these figures start, and the Courtesy Turn.

## The units in this class

| Unit | Call | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| [M17](../04-calls/m17-veer-left-veer-right.md) | Veer Left / Veer Right | moves a couple diagonally into a line |
| [M18](../04-calls/m18-star-thru.md) | Star Thru | turns a facing pair into a couple under an arch |
| [M19](../04-calls/m19-circulate-family.md) | Circulate Family | moves each dancer on to the next dancer's spot |
| [M20](../04-calls/m20-bend-the-line.md) | Bend the Line | folds two facing couples into a line, and back |

## Before you start

- They can Lead Right and finish facing the wall they led to.
- They can walk a line facing in and out without turning their shoulders early.
- They know that a couple has an inside hand and an outside hand.

## Teaching sequence

1. **M17 — Veer Left.** Take two couples to the middle and have them veer slowly while the class
   watches the elbow lead. Pre-empt the dancer who walks straight instead of arcing into the line.
2. **M18 — Star Thru.** Show the arch to the room: right hand to left, palm to palm, fingers up, at
   eye level. Walk the turn in pairs, then in facing couples. Pre-empt the pair who pull each other
   round; the arch is a hinge, not a handle.
3. **M19 — Circulate Family.** Everybody moves to the next dancer's spot along the path. Start with a
   single file, then a couples circulate. Pre-empt the couple who go the right way out of time.
4. **M20 — Bend the Line.** Point at the centre dancers: they step back, while the ends stand still and
   take hands. Pre-empt the end dancers bending too.

## Drills

1. Heads Lead Right
   Veer Left
   Couples Circulate
   Bend the Line
   Right and Left Thru
   Allemande Left
   Promenade Home

   Trains Veer into a line, the circulate along it, and Bend the Line from facing couples.

2. Heads Star Thru
   Heads Pass Thru
   Heads Star Thru
   Heads Pass Thru
   Allemande Left
   Promenade Home

   Trains Star Thru until the arch is automatic.

3. Heads Lead Right
   Veer Left
   Couples Circulate
   Bend the Line
   Pass Thru
   Allemande Left
   Promenade Home

   Trains the same line work with a pass through in the middle.

## Checks

- Veers fan out — steer the elbows before the line forms.
- Star Thru ends in a tangle — the arch is collapsing.
- The circulate box stretches — clap the beat while they walk it.
- Bend the Line leaves the line short — the centre pair do the moving.
