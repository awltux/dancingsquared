# Class 6 — Across and Back

> **Units covered:** M21, M22, M23, M24. **Units assumed:** M1–M20 — especially Star Thru, Veer and
> the circulate, because Square Thru finishes in the same box those calls work in.

## The units in this class

| Unit | Call | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| [M21](../04-calls/m21-square-thru.md) | Square Thru / Left Square Thru | passes two facing couples by alternating hands |
| [M22](../04-calls/m22-split-two.md) | Split Two | sends a couple between another couple |
| [M23](../04-calls/m23-grand-square.md) | Grand Square | walks the whole square in and out on four sides |
| [M24](../04-calls/m24-double-pass-thru.md) | Double Pass Thru | passes two facing couples in single file |

## Before you start

- They can Pass Thru and finish back to back, clear of each other.
- They can take and release a hand without steering anybody by it.
- They can stand still and hold a shape while another couple works around them.

## Teaching sequence

1. **M21 — Square Thru.** Teach one length only: Square Thru 4. Say the number out loud, then count
   the pull-bys as they walk. Pre-empt the dancers who turn to follow the released hand instead of
   turning a quarter in place.
2. **M22 — Split Two.** Put two couples in the middle and say that the dancers **not** named do nothing
   except open a gap. Pre-empt the inactive pair who close the gap too early.
3. **M23 — Grand Square.** Take it in eight-count sections, Heads and Sides separately before you put
   them together. Pre-empt the dancer who reverses direction early.
4. **M24 — Double Pass Thru.** Walk two couples past each other in single file, then have the ends turn
   in. Pre-empt the dancer who stops in the middle of the column.

## Drills

1. Heads Lead Right
   Circle Left 1/4
   Double Pass Thru
   First Couple Go Left, Next Couple Go Right
   Allemande Left
   Promenade Home

   Trains Double Pass Thru and the split that follows it.

2. Heads Square Thru 4
   Swing Thru
   Boys Trade
   Girls Trade
   Allemande Left
   Promenade Home

   Trains Square Thru into a wave, then the trades that put every dancer back in sequence.

3. Heads Square Thru 4
   Split Two
   Around 1 to a Line
   Forward and Back
   Pass Thru
   Allemande Left
   Promenade Home

   Trains Square Thru and Split Two, then the line that finishes the work.

## Checks

- Square Thru breaks after two hands — make them turn in place before the next pull-by.
- Split Two squeezes the active couple — the inactive pair closed the gap too soon.
- Grand Square falls apart in the second half — count the sections out loud.
- Double Pass Thru finishes side by side — send them through one behind the other.
