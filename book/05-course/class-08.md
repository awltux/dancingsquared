# Class 8 — Waves and the Box

> **Units covered:** M29, M30, M31, M32. **Units assumed:** M1–M28 — the class needs Bend the Line,
> Square Thru and Star Thru before any of these.

## The units in this class

| Unit | Call | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| [M29](../04-calls/m29-dive-thru.md) | Dive Thru | sends the centre dancers under an arch the others make |
| [M30](../04-calls/m30-box-the-gnat.md) | Box the Gnat | turns a facing pair by a pull, not an arch |
| [M31](../04-calls/m31-ocean-wave-family.md) | Ocean Wave Family | makes and balances waves, and the Alamo ring |
| [M32](../04-calls/m32-trade-family.md) | Trade Family | exchanges two dancers in a wave or a line |

## Before you start

- They can Bend the Line and know which pair does the moving.
- They can hold a hand at the right height for a wave, and let go when the call ends.
- They can turn a quarter in either direction on the spot without drifting.

## Teaching sequence

1. **M29 — Dive Thru.** Have two dancers make the arch and two dive under, with the class watching.
   Pre-empt the arch pair who stand up early and catch the divers across the back.
2. **M30 — Box the Gnat.** Put this beside Star Thru: same turn, different hands. Pre-empt the pair who
   arch — Box the Gnat is a pull by, and the hands stay low.
3. **M31 — Ocean Wave Family.** Build the wave slowly with one four-dancer group, then the whole
   square, and teach the Balance as part of the wave. Pre-empt dancers who turn their shoulders sideways.
4. **M32 — Trade Family.** Start with Partner Trade, one clear exchange, and only then the calls that
   name dancers. Pre-empt the dancer who walks round the outside instead of straight across.

## Drills

1. Heads Pass Thru
   Heads Dive Thru
   Allemande Left
   Promenade Home

   Trains Dive Thru from a facing line, and finishes the figure the way a tip usually finishes.

2. Heads Square Thru 4
   Partner Trade
   Allemande Left
   Promenade Home

   Trains the trade as a straight exchange that puts a column back in sequence.

3. Heads Pass the Ocean
   Balance
   Boys Trade
   Allemande Left
   Promenade Home

   Trains the wave, the balance, and a named-dancer trade inside one short figure.

## Checks

- Dive Thru ends in a pile — the arch came down early; hold it until the divers are through.
- Box the Gnat finishes like Star Thru — say "pull by, no hands up".
- The wave straightens into a line — keep the chests facing the centre.
- A trade leaves one dancer two steps out — step straight across, not round.
