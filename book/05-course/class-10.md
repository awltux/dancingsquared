# Class 10 — Wheels and Sweeps

> **Units covered:** M37, M38, M39, M40. **Units assumed:** M1–M36 — Square Thru and Bend the Line in
> particular, because the wheel and the sweep both come out of a line.

## The units in this class

| Unit | Call | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| [M37](../04-calls/m37-flutterwheel-reverse-flutterwheel.md) | Flutterwheel / Reverse Flutterwheel | wheels the two halves of a circle past each other |
| [M38](../04-calls/m38-sweep-a-quarter.md) | Sweep a Quarter | turns a two-couple group a quarter, away from the centre |
| [M39](../04-calls/m39-trade-by.md) | Trade By | passes two facing couples while the others trade |
| [M40](../04-calls/m40-ferris-wheel.md) | Ferris Wheel | turns two facing couples right over, as a wheel |

## Before you start

- They can Wheel and Deal as a couple, and know who pivots.
- They can Bend the Line and finish in a proper two-faced line.
- They hold hands at the right height and let go cleanly on a pass.

## Teaching sequence

1. **M37 — Flutterwheel.** Show it with two couples only: the two dancers on the same side take hands
   and go round together, then each turns to the other's partner. Pre-empt the pair who let go halfway
   round; the wheel turns on that handhold.
2. **M38 — Sweep a Quarter.** Contrast it with Flutterwheel: nothing is exchanged, the group just turns
   a quarter. Pre-empt the dancers who turn towards the middle; the sweep moves away.
3. **M39 — Trade By.** Teach it as a half Square Thru with a trade on the outside. Pre-empt the dancer
   who walks round the outside instead of straight into their opposite's spot.
4. **M40 — Ferris Wheel.** Take the hands, look at your partner, and turn the pair right over. Pre-empt
   the four dancers turning as individuals, which makes four quarter turns instead of one roll.

## Drills

1. Heads Pass Thru
   Heads Flutterwheel
   Heads Sweep a Quarter
   Heads Pass Thru
   Allemande Left
   Promenade Home

   Trains Flutterwheel and Sweep a Quarter side by side, so the class hears the difference.

2. Heads Pass Thru
   Heads Trade By
   Heads Square Thru 4
   Allemande Left
   Promenade Home

   Trains Trade By inside a figure the class already knows.

3. Heads Square Thru 4
   Ferris Wheel
   Centers In
   Cast Off Three Quarters
   Allemande Left
   Promenade Home

   Trains the Ferris Wheel as a full roll, then brings the square back into shape for the close.

## Checks

- Flutterwheel ends with the couples apart — the pair let go; the wheel needs both hands joined.
- Sweep a Quarter turns the wrong way — sweep away from the centre.
- Trade By leaves two dancers facing out — only half the square passes.
- Ferris Wheel is four quarter turns — take the handhold first and look at your partner.
