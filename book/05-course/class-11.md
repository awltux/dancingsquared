# Class 11 — Quarters and Centres

> **Units covered:** M41, M42, M43, M44, M45. **Units assumed:** M1–M40 — this class leans on the
> wave, the line and the wheel from every class so far.

## The units in this class

| Unit | Call | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| [M41](../04-calls/m41-zoom.md) | Zoom | walks the lead dancer round the box into the trailer's spot |
| [M42](../04-calls/m42-touch-a-quarter.md) | Touch a Quarter | steps two facing dancers into a wave |
| [M43](../04-calls/m43-hinge-family.md) | Hinge Family | turns a pair a quarter in place |
| [M44](../04-calls/m44-centers-in.md) | Centers In | steps the centre dancers in to make a wave |
| [M45](../04-calls/m45-cast-off-three-quarters.md) | Cast Off Three Quarters | turns the outside dancers three quarters round |

## Before you start

- They can tell a centre from an end without looking round the square.
- They can move into a wave without pushing their opposite out of position.
- They can turn exactly a quarter and stop on the spot.

## Teaching sequence

1. **M41 — Zoom.** Say "leaders roll, trailers walk" and have one box demonstrate. Pre-empt the pair
   who both move: the leader rolls a full circle, the trailer walks straight.
2. **M42 — Touch a Quarter.** Teach the touch before the turn, because the turn is the easy half.
   Pre-empt the pair whose wave is too wide to hold.
3. **M43 — Hinge Family.** The smallest call in the course, and dancers rush it. Hold the pair in place
   and count the quarter out loud. Pre-empt the dancer who turns further than asked.
4. **M44 — Centers In.** Point at the centre pair, then at the ends, and have the ends stay still.
   Pre-empt the end dancers who step in as well.
5. **M45 — Cast Off Three Quarters.** Count it in quarters as the outside pair walks. Pre-empt the
   dancer who turns a half or a full by mistake.

## Drills

1. Heads Touch a Quarter
   Single Hinge
   Centers In
   Cast Off Three Quarters
   Allemande Left
   Promenade Home

   Trains four small quarter turns in a row, which the class has to do without thinking.

2. Heads Pass Thru
   Heads Square Thru 4
   Zoom
   Centers In
   Allemande Left
   Promenade Home

   Trains Zoom from a column, then the call that finishes it.

3. Heads Pass the Ocean
   Swing Thru
   Boys Trade
   Centers In
   Allemande Left
   Promenade Home

   Trains the Class 9 wave calls with the centres call from this class.

## Checks

- Zoom leaves the box stretched — the trailer walked too far or the leader cut the circle short.
- Touch a Quarter ends with no wave — touch first, then turn.
- Hinge overshoots — count the quarter aloud.
- Centers In drags the ends in too — say who moves and who stands still.
- Cast Off Three Quarters finishes at the wrong wall — count the quarters as they walk.
