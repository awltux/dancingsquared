# Class 12 — The Last Calls

> **Units covered:** M46, M47, M48, M49, M50. **Units assumed:** all fifty units before them — every
> figure here is built from calls the class already knows.

## The units in this class

| Unit | Call | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| [M46](../04-calls/m46-spin-the-top.md) | Spin the Top | turns the top of a wave, then the rest of it |
| [M47](../04-calls/m47-scoot-back.md) | Scoot Back | sends the ends and the centres past each other |
| [M48](../04-calls/m48-fold-cross-fold.md) | Fold / Cross Fold | folds one dancer in beside the other |
| [M49](../04-calls/m49-tag-the-line-family.md) | Tag the Line | walks a column forward and turns it into a line |
| [M50](../04-calls/m50-recycle.md) | Recycle (from Waves only) | turns a wave into a couple facing in |

## Before you start

- They can hold a wave and turn it without losing the handhold.
- They can Fold and Roll as individuals, and know which dancer stays put.
- They can walk a column forward in time, and stop together.

## Teaching sequence

1. **M46 — Spin the Top.** Build it from a wave in front of the class, naming the top pair first. Walk
   it without the turn so they see the two halves, then add the turn. Pre-empt the pair who turn before
   the other pair moves.
2. **M47 — Scoot Back.** Say "ends in, centres out" and demonstrate once with one wave. Pre-empt the
   dancer who steps straight forward instead of into the vacated spot.
3. **M48 — Fold / Cross Fold.** Teach it from a wave, and name which dancer folds. Pre-empt the pair
   who both fold: the dancer not named stays put.
4. **M49 — Tag the Line.** Walk the column forward counting the steps, then call the direction at the
   end. Pre-empt the line turning too early.
5. **M50 — Recycle.** Teach it from a wave only, as the manual allows. Pre-empt the pair who turn away
   from each other: the centres turn inwards and the ends fold round them.

## Drills

1. Heads Pass Thru
   Heads Square Thru 4
   Spin the Top
   Scoot Back
   Allemande Left
   Promenade Home

   Trains the two wave calls of this class back to back.

2. Heads Pass Thru
   Heads Square Thru 4
   Tag the Line
   Allemande Left
   Promenade Home

   Trains Tag the Line out of a column, with the line turning in as it arrives.

3. Heads Pass Thru
   Heads Square Thru 4
   Spin the Top
   Recycle
   Allemande Left
   Promenade Home

   Trains the last new call in a figure built from what came before it.

## Checks

- Spin the Top stalls halfway — name the top pair out loud.
- Scoot Back leaves two dancers facing the wrong way — ends and centres went the wrong directions.
- Fold leaves nobody in the vacated spot — only the named dancer moves.
- Tag the Line turns too early — walk the column first, then call the turn.
- Recycle finishes face to face — turn inward and let the ends fold round.
