# Drills

A drill is a short call sequence that puts two or three calls together and then stops. Dance it
slowly until the shapes are right, then dance it again to music.

Every drill here starts from a squared set, except where the note says otherwise, and uses only calls
taught in that class or earlier. The class pages carry their own drills; this is the graded set, one
per class, with a warm-up at the start and a get-out at the end.

## Warm-up

1. Circle Left 1/4
   Dosado
   Allemande Left
   Promenade Home

   Trains the first evening's calls and gets the square moving before anything new arrives.

## Class 1 — the ring and the allemande

1. Circle Left (Full)
   Forward and Back
   Dosado
   Allemande Left
   Promenade Home

   Trains the ring at full length, then the same calls after a movement that changes only the timing.

## Class 2 — stars and the grand

1. Circle Left 1/4
   Allemande Left
   Right and Left Grand
   Promenade Home

   Trains the allemande-to-grand join that closes almost every tip.

## Class 3 — the couple calls

1. Heads Right and Left Thru
   Heads Ladies Chain
   Heads Swing
   Promenade Home

   Trains the three couple calls in the order a caller most often uses them.

## Class 4 — passing and leading

1. Heads Lead Right
   Heads Half Sashay
   Heads U-Turn Back
   Heads Lead Left
   Allemande Left
   Promenade Home

   Trains the turn-and-lead calls with the sashay between them.

## Class 5 — veer, Star Thru and the circulate

1. Heads Lead Right
   Veer Left
   Couples Circulate
   Bend the Line
   Right and Left Thru
   Allemande Left
   Promenade Home

   Trains Veer into a line, the circulate along it, and Bend the Line from facing couples.

## Class 6 — across and back

1. Heads Square Thru 4
   Swing Thru
   Boys Trade
   Girls Trade
   Allemande Left
   Promenade Home

   Trains Square Thru into a wave, then the trades that put every dancer back in sequence.

## Class 7 — lines and turns

1. Heads Double Pass Thru
   First Couple Go Left, Next Couple Go Right
   California Twirl
   Pass Thru
   Wheel and Deal
   Double Pass Thru
   Allemande Left
   Promenade Home

   Trains the split column, the twirl and the wheel, and finishes with a pass through that returns the
   square.

## Class 8 — waves and the box

1. Heads Pass Thru
   Heads Dive Thru
   Allemande Left
   Promenade Home

   Trains Dive Thru from a facing line, and finishes the figure the way a tip usually finishes.

## Class 9 — waves in motion

1. Heads Pass Thru
   Heads Pass the Ocean
   Swing Thru
   Extend
   Allemande Left
   Promenade Home

   Trains Pass the Ocean, the swing through the wave, and the extend that moves the wave along.

## Class 10 — wheels and sweeps

1. Heads Pass Thru
   Heads Flutterwheel
   Heads Sweep a Quarter
   Heads Pass Thru
   Allemande Left
   Promenade Home

   Trains Flutterwheel and Sweep a Quarter side by side, so the class hears the difference.

## Class 11 — quarters and centres

1. Heads Touch a Quarter
   Single Hinge
   Centers In
   Cast Off Three Quarters
   Allemande Left
   Promenade Home

   Trains four small quarter turns in a row, which is what the class has to do without thinking.

## Class 12 — the last calls

1. Heads Pass Thru
   Heads Square Thru 4
   Spin the Top
   Scoot Back
   Allemande Left
   Promenade Home

   Trains the two wave calls of the last class back to back, and brings the square home.

## Get-out

1. Allemande Left
   Right and Left Grand
   Promenade Home

   Trains the standard get-out: allemande to corner, grand right and left, promenade. Use it whenever
   you want a figure to finish cleanly.
