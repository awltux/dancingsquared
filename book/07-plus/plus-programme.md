# The Plus program

The **Plus Program** is the next CALLERLAB program after Mainstream. This page is an orientation, not
a course — the definitions themselves are CALLERLAB's *Plus Program Definitions*, revised
**25 August 2026**.

## How the calls are grouped

Unlike Mainstream, the Plus manual has **no suggested teaching order**. Its definitions appear in
alphabetical order, and this page follows the manual's own order. If you are learning Plus, your
teacher or caller will choose a route; the calls fall naturally into a few groups.

### Calls built on what you already know

These are the ones that will feel familiar from the first attempt:

- **Acey Deucey** — the circulate family, extended
- **Fan the Top**, **Grand Swing Thru**, **Relay the Deucey** — the ocean wave family
- **Ping Pong Circulate**, **Diamond Circulate**, **Linear Cycle** — circulates again
- **Triple Trade**, **Trade the Wave** — the trade family
- **Partner Hinge**, **Partner Tag** — the hinge and tag families
- **Dixie Grand** — a grand-square-shaped figure with a swing
- **Do Paso** — an allemande-based figure you may already have met as a variation

### Calls with a new shape

- **Chase Right** — the right-hand two-faced line's own call
- **Circle to a Line** — out of a circle and into a line
- **Cloverleaf**, **Clover and *anything*** — the clover shapes
- **Coordinate**, **Crossfire**, **Cut the Diamond**, **Flip the Diamond**
- **Linear Cycle**, **Load the Boat**, **Peel Off**, **Peel the Top**
- **Pass the Sea**, **Pass to the Center**
- **Shoot the Star**, **Slip the Clutch**, **Teacup Chain**, **Track 2**
- **Spin Chain Thru**, **Spin Chain the Gears**, **Spin Chain and Exchange the Gears**

### The *Anything* calls

Two Plus calls are wrappers with a slot in them:

- **(anything) and Roll**
- **(anything) and Spread**
- **Explode and (anything)**
- **Clover and (anything)**

The idea is the one from [Concepts and modifiers](../02-language/concepts-and-modifiers.md): the
dancers complete the first part, then complete whatever the caller names in the slot.

### Families and formations

- **Quarter Thru Family** — Quarter Thru and its relatives
- **Thar Family** — calls from a Thar
- **See Saw**, **Turn Thru**, **Walk and Dodge**, **Walk Around the Corner**
- **All 8 Spin the Top**, **Eight Chain Thru**, **Follow Your Neighbor**
- **Pair Off**, **Scoot and Dodge**, **Triple Scoot**

## What Plus expects of you

Three things, and they are all about the Mainstream calls rather than the new ones:

1. **Formations, instantly.** Fan the Top and Grand Swing Thru assume you know a wave without looking
   at your feet.
2. **The [Facing Couples Rule](../03-rules/facing-couples-rule.md), generalised.** At Plus it applies
   to more calls and more formations.
3. **Circulate paths.** A large fraction of Plus calls are circulates in disguise.
