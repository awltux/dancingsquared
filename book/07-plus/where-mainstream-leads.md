# Where Mainstream leads

Once you can dance the 50 Mainstream calls comfortably, the next program is **Plus**. This short
appendix is not a Plus course — it is a look at the doorway, and at exactly what changes when you
walk through it.

## The programs

```
Mainstream   →   Plus   →   A1   →   A2   →   C1 …
```

Each program includes everything below it. A Plus dance contains Mainstream calls, and the caller
expects you to know them without thinking.

## What Plus actually changes

The Plus manual devotes a section to it, headed **"Mainstream Restrictions that are Removed at
Plus"**. It is the most useful thing in the document for a Mainstream dancer, because it says
precisely what you are currently *not* allowed to do.

### 1. Courtesy Turn, and everything built on it

> "In the Mainstream Program, Courtesy Turn can start only from a Normal Couple. At Plus this
> restriction is removed. As a result, the Mainstream restrictions on Right and Left Thru and on
> Chain Down the Line are also removed."

In other words: the call you have learned as "only from a Normal Couple" becomes general. The
[courtesy turn restriction](../03-rules/mainstream-restrictions.md) is the one that catches Mainstream
dancers most often, and Plus removes it.

### 2. Pass the Ocean

> "In the Mainstream Program, Pass the Ocean can start only from Facing Couples. At Plus this
> restriction is removed. Therefore, at Plus it is proper to call Pass the Ocean from a Right-Hand
> Ocean Wave and from other formations allowed by the Ocean Wave Rule."

### 3. Swing Thru and Left Swing Thru

> "In the Mainstream Program, the starting formation for Swing Thru or Left Swing Thru must be either
> a Wave of at least 3 dancers or an Alamo Ring. At Plus it is also proper for the starting formation
> to be a Right-Hand or Left-Hand Thar."

### 4. The Facing Couples Rule is generalised

At Mainstream the [Facing Couples Rule](../03-rules/facing-couples-rule.md) applies to specific
calls. At Plus it is widened to **any formation where dancers can step forward to make a wave**:

| You are in | You step into |
|---|---|
| Facing Dancers | a Mini-Wave |
| Facing Couples | an Ocean Wave |
| Facing Lines | a Tidal Wave |
| an Eight Chain Thru formation | Parallel Waves |
| a Right and Left Grand circle | a Thar |

And it applies to a named list of Plus calls, plus three Mainstream calls you already know —
**Swing Thru**, **Left Swing Thru** and **Spin the Top**.

## What that tells you about Mainstream

Read the other way round, the same section is a description of what you are learning now:

- your Courtesy Turn is deliberately narrow, and it is meant to be;
- your Pass the Ocean is a facing-couples call, and calling it from a wave is not proper yet;
- your Swing Thru needs a wave of three or an Alamo Ring.

A beginner who knows these three restrictions will avoid most of the avoidable failures of their
first year, because these are exactly the calls a caller reaches for when a square is in an unusual
shape.

## How Mainstream calls reappear

Plus does not throw anything away. The Mainstream calls come back in new combinations:

- the [circulate family](../04-calls/m19-circulate-family.md) is the basis of several Plus calls
  (Ping Pong Circulate, Diamond Circulate, Linear Cycle)
- the [trade family](../04-calls/m32-trade-family.md) reappears as Triple Trade and Trade the Wave
- the ocean wave family grows into Fan the Top, Grand Swing Thru and Relay the Deucey
- concepts like *Anything* and *Split* extend rather than change

Which means: dancing Plus well is mostly a matter of knowing Mainstream *really* well.
