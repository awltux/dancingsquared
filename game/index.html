How to play

The two boards are two moments of one square. The left board is where you are; the right one is the arrangement you are trying to reach. Every question is one call that gets you there.

The Drill
The season's calls in order, ten to a level. Each drill is one call; answer it and the next one is yours. A cell you have passed can be reopened to watch its calls back, and a cell you closed without a pass can be attempted again.
Today's Daily
One puzzle, the same for everyone, once a day. Its clock starts the first time you open it, and the time you spend away from the page is charged to the run — so a Daily is a sitting rather than something to leave running.
Two-group steps
Some calls only happen when two groups do different things at once. Hold Shift and click a call to begin a two-group step, then pick a call under another group. The line above the answers says which reading is armed, and pressing it again puts the reading down.
Stars
Three stars is a clean run at par: no hint, no wrong answer, the shortest line, and no slower than par. Losing either half of that — a wasted call, or a time over par — is two stars, and losing both is one. Being solved is never zero.
Hints
One is earned every ten solved drills, and a hint spends a minute of the Daily's clock as well as a hint. The three dots beside the hint button are what you hold, filled for what is there.
Signing in
Without an account you can play everything; your runs are kept on this device and are not sent anywhere, so there is nothing to rank. Signing in hands those runs to the server as history — kept, and not re-ranked — and from then on a run is scored against the drill it was served with.

The banner is on every page: the way back on the left, and what you hold and what you are worth beside it.

Your account

An account puts your runs on the server, which is what lets them be ranked and what puts your name on the board. Without one the game is complete and everything stays on this device.
